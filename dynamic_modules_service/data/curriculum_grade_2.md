# Grade 2 Curriculum & Speech Targets (Ages 6–7)
**Curriculum Guide**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 2 Curriculum Guide (pp. 47–60)
**Key Stage**: Key Stage 1 (Word Study & Sentence Construction)

---

## 1. DepEd MATATAG Competencies
* **EN2PA-I-1**: Recognize rhymes in chants, poems, and stories heard (L, S).
* **EN2PA-I-2**: Segment onset and rime (L, S).
* **EN2PWS-I-1**: Identify Grade 2 level-appropriate sight words (L, S, R, W).
* **EN2PWS-I-2**: Identify alphabet letter names (L, S, R, W).
* **EN2PWS-I-3**: Read words accurately and automatically according to word patterns (initial, final, medial): **CVC, CVCe, CVVC, CVCC, CCVC** words (L, S, R, W).

---

## 2. Speech & Phonological Focus
* **ASHA Targets**: Liquids `/l, r/`, fricative `/v/`, affricates `/tʃ, dʒ/`, R-clusters (`/br, tr, dr, kr, ɡr/`), final devoicing resolution.
* **Clinical Focus**: Word-level fluency -> Carrier phrases -> Sentence construction -> Connected conversational speech.

---

## 3. Island Gameplay Maps (Grade 2 / Ages 6–7)

* **Speech Targets**: R-Clusters (`/tr, dr, br/`), Affricates `/tʃ, dʒ/`

| Level | MATATAG Focus | Speech Target | 5-Stage Mechanic | Target Vocabulary & Phrases |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1.1 Village Signpost** | EN2PWS-I-3 (CVCe patterns) | /tʃ/ ("ch"), /dʒ/ ("j") | **Step 3 - Build & Say** | `chair`, `chain`, `juice`, `bridge`, `jacket`. |
| **1.2 Rime Match Cabin** | EN2PA-I-2 (Onset & rime) | R-Clusters (`/tr, dr/`) | **Step 2 - Find the Word** | `train`, `tree`, `drum`, `dress`, `truck`. |
| **1.3 River Crossing** | EN2PWS-I-1 (Sight words) | Liquid /r/ in clusters | **Step 4 - Build the Bridge** | `brown`, `bread`, `brush`, `brick`. |
