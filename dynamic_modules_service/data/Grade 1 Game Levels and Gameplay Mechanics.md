# Grade 1 Game Levels & Gameplay Mechanics

Grade 1 levels and gameplay mechanics continue below, structured using the MATATAG Grade 1 Reading, Literacy, and Language curricula alongside ASHA speech sound acquisition targets for ages 5–6.

## Island 1: "My Self & My Family" (Quarter 1)

- **MATATAG Focus:** Self and family vocabulary, sound isolation (initial/final), rhyming, and syllable segmentation.
- **ASHA Target (Age 5):** Liquid /l/ acquisition, eliminating gliding (/l/ → /w, y/), and resolving deaffrication (/ch/ → /sh/).

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1: Family Name & Sound Station | Self & family vocabulary (RL1VWK-I-1); initial sound isolation (RL1PWS-I-3). | Liquid /l/ production; eliminating Gliding (/l/ → /w/). | SequenceJumperWidget (Letter Assembly): Drag letter tiles onto target frames to spell family words. | Default: Love, Like, Leaf, Line. Gliding Target: /l/ words with visual lip positioning cue ("Love" vs. "Wove"). |
| 1.2: Family Sorting Basket | Classifying naming words for family/persons (RL1VWK-I-2a). | Affricate /ch/ production; eliminating Deaffrication. | BucketSortWidget (Category Bin): Drag family action/item cards into correct role bins. | Default: Chair, Child, Check, Hug. Deaffrication Target: /ch/ vs. /sh/ contrasts (Chair vs. Share, Chop vs. Shop). |
| 1.3: Rhyme Time Porch | Identifying & producing rhyming words (RL1PA-I-3, RL1PA-I-4). | Liquid /l/ in final positions & cluster prep. | MinimalPairWidget (Flip & Match): Flip shutters to pair rhyming words. | Default: Ball–Tall, Wall–Fall, Bell–Well. Error Target: Minimal pairs contrasting /l/ omissions (Ball vs. Bo). |
| 1.4: Echo Family Studio | Narrating personal family experiences (RL1CCT-I-1a). | Connected speech fluency & liquid sound integration. | EchoCaveWidget (Mic Recording): Record 2–3 word sentence prompts. | Default: "I love family", "Like my home". Target Profile: Sentences targeting /l/ and /ch/ accuracy. |

## Island 2: "School Life & Class Routines" (Quarter 2)

- **MATATAG Focus:** School vocabulary, describing words, story sequencing (3 events), cause and effect.
- **ASHA Target (Age 5):** Liquid /r/ acquisition, eliminating gliding (/r/ → /w/), and mastering L-Clusters (/bl, gl, fl/).

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1: Classroom Item Catcher | School objects & describing words (RL1VWK-II-1, RL1VWK-II-2b). | Liquid /r/ production; eliminating Gliding (/r/ → /w/). | TapAndPopWidget (Target Hunter): Tap falling classroom supplies containing target sounds. | Default: Red, Ruler, Ring, Eraser. Gliding Target: /r/ targets with tongue-retraction visual cues (Red vs. Wed). |
| 2.2: Story Line Jumper | Narrative sequencing with 3 chronological events (RL1CAT-II-1b). | L-Consonant Blends (/bl, gl, fl/); eliminating Cluster Reduction. | SequenceJumperWidget (Card Ordering): Arrange 3 story sequence cards in chronological order. | Default: First (Bell), Second (Read), Third (Play). Blend Target: Blend targets bolded (Blue book, Flag raised, Glass clean). |
| 2.3: Cause & Effect Lab | Identifying cause & effect in school events (RL1CAT-II-1f). | Voiced fricative /v/ production & medial /r/. | BucketSortWidget (Cause-Effect Bin): Match cause cards to their effect bins. | Default: Rain → Wet, Read → Learn. Target Profile: Words containing /v/ and /r/ (Give, Very, Read, Write). |
| 2.4: Echo Classroom Express | Retelling stories & expressing school ideas (RL1CCT-II-2, RL1CCT-II-3a). | Connected speech & multisyllabic sentence articulation. | EchoCaveWidget (Speech Studio): Record full short phrases into the microphone. | Default: "I read books", "My red ruler". Target Profile: Embedded /r/ and L-blend phrases ("Blue ribbon is red"). |

## Island 3: "Community Helpers & Places" (Quarter 3)

- **MATATAG Focus:** Community helpers/places, sentence reading fluency, problem and solution, informational texts.
- **ASHA Target (Ages 5–6):** Affricates /ch, j/, fricative /v/, eliminating R-Cluster Reduction (/tr, dr, kr, gr/).

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1: Community Map Navigator | Community helpers & places (RL1VWK-III-1); labeling occupations. | Voiced affricate /j/ production; eliminating Deaffrication (/j/ → /zh, z/). | BucketSortWidget (Map Sort): Drag helper cards to designated community locations. | Default: Judge, Job, Jump, Guard. Deaffrication Target: /j/ target emphasis (Judge, Jump, Jam) with mouth diagram. |
| 3.2: Helper's Task Trail | Reading simple sentences with speed & accuracy (RL1CAT-III-1). | R-Consonant Blends (/tr, dr, kr, gr/); eliminating Cluster Reduction. | SequenceJumperWidget (Step Path): Hop across path tiles by reading short task sentences. | Default: Train, Drive, Truck, Green. Cluster Target: R-blends highlighted (Truck vs. Tuck, Drive vs. Dive). |
| 3.3: Problem-Solver Market | Identifying problem & solution in informational texts (RL1CAT-III-3b). | Labiodental fricative /v/ & palatal /sh/ review. | TapAndPopWidget (Bubble Grab): Pop solution bubbles to solve community problems. | Default: Van, Vest, Village, Doctor. Error Target: Contrast pairs for /v/ vs. /b/ (Van vs. Ban, Vest vs. Best). |
| 3.4: Radio Station Echo | Narrating community experiences (RL1CCT-III-1a). | Complex sentence production & acoustic speech self-monitoring. | EchoCaveWidget (Broadcast Mic): Record news reports for community helpers. | Default: "Drive the truck", "Visit the clinic". Target Profile: Sentences with /j/, /v/, and R-clusters. |

## Island 4: "Our Environment & Nature Explorer" (Quarter 4)

- **MATATAG Focus:** Environment vocabulary, informational text comprehension, creative response, expressing environmental ideas.
- **ASHA Target (Age 6):** Interdental fricatives /th/ (θ, ð), S-Clusters (/st, sp, sk/), and resolving epenthesis/stopping.

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1: Nature Trail Cleaner | Environmental terms & describing natural resources (RL1VWK-IV-1, RL1VWK-IV-2b). | Voiceless interdental /th/ (θ); eliminating Stopping (/th/ → /t, p/). | TapAndPopWidget (Eco-Pop): Tap environmental litter items containing target sounds. | Default: Earth, Think, Path, Tree. Stopping Target: Tongue-between-teeth visual guide for /th/ (Think vs. Tink). |
| 4.2: Ecosystem Sequence Jump | Sequencing environmental events & cause-effect relationships (RL1CAT-IV-2b, f). | Voiced interdental /ð/; eliminating Stopping (/ð/ → /d/). | SequenceJumperWidget (Path Hop): Jump across logs by following plant/animal growth stages. | Default: This, That, They, Mother Nature. Stopping Target: Contrast pairs for /ð/ vs. /d/ (This vs. Dis, That vs. Dat). |
| 4.3: Earth Defender Match | Informational text details & creative response (RL1CAT-IV-3a, RL1CCT-IV-4). | S-Consonant Blends (/st, sp, sk/); eliminating Cluster Reduction. | MinimalPairWidget (Card Flip): Match eco-actions to environmental impact cards. | Default: Star, Sky, Plant, Stone. Cluster Target: S-blend contrasts (Star vs. Tar, Sky vs. Ky, Spoon vs. Poon). |
| 4.4: Explorer's Echo Cave | Expressing ideas & narrating personal experiences with nature (RL1CCT-IV-1a, 3a). | Complex connected speech integration & phonetic habituation. | EchoCaveWidget (Cave Recording): Record complete environmental pledges. | Default: "I protect the earth", "Plant green trees". Target Profile: Custom phrases matching identified Grade 1 error targets. |

## Island 5: "Grade 1 Mastery Island" (Post-Assessment)

**Purpose:** Interactive post-assessment embedded directly into gameplay mechanics to evaluate Grade 1 speech sound mastery and curriculum-aligned literacy competencies without formal testing stress.

| Level & Name | Diagnostic Screener Objective | Phonological Target & Process Tested | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Assessment Target Word / Phrase Items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1: Echo River | Re-evaluates Liquid sounds (/l, r/) and Gliding processes. | Liquid production (/l, r/) & Gliding elimination. | EchoCaveWidget (River Echo): Tap floating leaves, listen to prompt, repeat target into mic. | Target Items: Leaf, Love, Red, Ring, Ruler, Yellow. |
| 5.2: Mirror Cave | Re-evaluates Affricates (/ch, j/) and Fricatives (/v, sh/). | Deaffrication, Fronting, and Stopping of Fricatives. | TapAndPopWidget + EchoCaveWidget (Crystal Wipe & Record): Wipe crystal, state revealed word into mic. | Target Items: Chair, Chop, Judge, Jump, Van, Vest. |
| 5.3: Cluster Bridge Challenge | Re-evaluates complex consonant blends (R-blends, L-blends, S-blends). | Cluster Reduction across /bl, fl, tr, dr, st, sk/ blends. | SequenceJumperWidget + EchoCaveWidget (Bridge Step & Speak): Cross bridge planks by pronouncing blend targets. | Target Items: Blue, Flag, Truck, Drive, Star, Sky. |
| 5.4: Grand Narrative Speech Ceremony | Evaluates overall sentence fluency, speech intelligibility, and Grade 1 connected oral discourse. | Connected narrative speech articulation & sentence-level PCC. | EchoCaveWidget (Storybook Narration): Turn digital storybook pages and record 3 summary sentences. | Target Sentences: 1. "I love my family and school." 2. "The red truck drives fast." 3. "We protect the green earth." |
