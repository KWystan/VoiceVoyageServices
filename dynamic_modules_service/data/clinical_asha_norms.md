# Clinical Speech Sound Acquisition Norms & Phonological Process Definitions
**Primary Clinical Benchmarks**: Crowe, K., & McLeod, S. (2020). Children's English Consonant Acquisition in the United States: A Review. *AJSLP*, 29, 2155–2169; McLeod, S., & Crowe, K. (2018). *AJSLP*, 27(4), 1546–1571.
**Diagnostic Criterion**: 90% mastery threshold across 18,907 children.

---

## 1. Age-Normative Consonant Acquisition Thresholds (90% Mastery)

| Age Bracket | Acquired Consonants (90% Criterion) | Clinical Screening Role in Voice Voyage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Age 2 (24–35 mos)** | **/p, b, m, d, n, h, w/** | Baseline early stops, nasals, and glides. Omissions at word onset signal Initial Consonant Deletion (ICD). |
| **Age 3 (36–47 mos)** | **/t, k, ɡ, f, v, ŋ/** | Velar stops and labiodental fricatives. Velar Fronting (`/k, ɡ/ -> [t, d]`) and Prevocalic Voicing resolve. |
| **Age 4 (48–59 mos)** | **/j, l, dʒ, tʃ, s, ʃ, z/** | Sibilants, affricates, and liquid /l/. Stopping of `/s, z, ʃ/`, Weak Syllable Deletion, and Palatal Fronting resolve. |
| **Age 5 (60–71 mos)** | **/r, ʒ/** | Rhotic liquid /r/ and palatal fricative /ʒ/. Gliding (`/r/ -> [w]`) and Deaffrication resolve. |
| **Age 6 (72–83 mos)** | **/ð/** ("voiced th", *this*, *feather*) | Voiced interdental fricative mastery. Vowelization of post-vocalic liquids resolves. |
| **Ages 7–8 (84–96+ mos)** | **/θ/** ("voiceless th", *thumb*, *bath*) | Voiceless interdental fricative mastery. Developmentally normative up to age 8. |

## 3. Core Articulatory Shift Definitions

- **Weak Syllable Deletion (WSD)**: Omits an unstressed syllable (*"banana"* $\rightarrow$ *[næ.nə]*). Reflects developing prosodic rhythm control.
- **Cluster Reduction**: Deletes a consonant from a blend (*"star"* $\rightarrow$ *[tɑɹ]*, *"blue"* $\rightarrow$ *[bu]*). Reduces syllable complexity.
- **Final Consonant Deletion (FCD)**: Omits word-final coda consonant (*"cat"* $\rightarrow$ *[kæ]*). Reverts to simple open CV shape.
- **Initial Consonant Deletion (ICD)**: Omits single word-initial consonant (*"pig"* $\rightarrow$ *[ɪɡ]*). Defies universal consonant onset preferences; strong clinical marker for apraxia/disorder.
- **Medial Consonant Deletion (MCD)**: Omits intervocalic consonant (*"paper"* $\rightarrow$ *[peɪ.ɚ]*).
- **Reduplication**: Clones first syllable onto second syllable (*"water"* $\rightarrow$ *[wɑ.wɑ]*). Early toddler motor simplification.
- **Stopping**: Replaces continuous friction with a stop closure (*"sun"* $\rightarrow$ *[tʌn]*, *"fish"* $\rightarrow$ *[pɪʃ]*).
- **Deaffrication**: Collapses two-part affricate (/tʃ, dʒ/) into single stop or fricative (*"chair"* $\rightarrow$ *[ʃɛɹ]* or *[tɛɹ]*).
- **Fronting (Velar)**: Shifts velar /k, ɡ/ forward to alveolar /t, d/ (*"car"* $\rightarrow$ *[tɑɹ]*).
- **Palatal Fronting**: Shifts palatal /ʃ/ forward to alveolar [s, t] (*"shoe"* $\rightarrow$ *[su]*).
- **Backing**: Unnatural backward shift of anterior /t, d, s/ to posterior /k, ɡ/ (*"dog"* $\rightarrow$ *[ɡɔɡ]*).
- **Gliding**: Substitutes difficult liquids /l, r/ with easy glides /w, j/ (*"red"* $\rightarrow$ *[wɛd]*).
- **Liquidization**: Unnatural substitution of liquid for glide (*"water"* $\rightarrow$ *[ɹɔ.tɚ]*).
- **Vowelization**: Replaces coda liquid with vocalic resonance (*"ball"* $\rightarrow$ *[bɔ.o]*).
- **Frication**: Unnatural replacement of stop with fricative (*"two"* $\rightarrow$ *[su]*).
- **Denasalization**: Failure of velopharyngeal opening during nasals (*"moon"* $\rightarrow$ *[bun]*).
- **Prevocalic Voicing**: Early vocal fold vibration before vowel onset (*"pie"* $\rightarrow$ *[baɪ]*).
- **Devoicing / Final Devoicing**: Premature cessation of voicing at word boundaries (*"bed"* $\rightarrow$ *[bɛt]*).
- **Consonant Harmony**: Consonant adapts place/manner of another consonant in the word (*"duck"* $\rightarrow$ *[ɡʌk]*).

## 4. ASHA Clinical Priority Hierarchy (`_apply_clinical_hierarchy`)

When a single phoneme error satisfies multiple detector criteria (e.g. `/tʃ/ -> [t]` matching both *Deaffrication* and *Stopping*), the system applies this 15-tier clinical priority ranking to retain only the most diagnostically relevant primary error:

1. **Weak Syllable Deletion** *(Whole-syllable prosodic level)*
2. **Stopping** *(Severe manner error)*
3. **Frication** *(Atypical manner error)*
4. **Deaffrication** *(Affricate reduction)*
5. **Denasalization** *(Resonance error)*
6. **Fronting** *(Place error: posterior $\rightarrow$ anterior)*
7. **Backing** *(Atypical place error: anterior $\rightarrow$ posterior)*
8. **Gliding** *(Liquid manner reduction)*
9. **Liquidization** *(Atypical glide-to-liquid error)*
10. **Vowelization** *(Liquid to vocalic resonance)*
11. **Prevocalic Voicing** *(Voice onset timing error)*
12. **Devoicing / Final Devoicing** *(Coda voicing cessation)*
13. **Cluster Reduction** *(Syllable structure deletion)*
14. **Final Consonant Deletion** *(Word-final syllable closure)*
15. **Consonant Deletion** *(Singleton consonant omission)*
