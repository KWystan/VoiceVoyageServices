# Grade 1 Curriculum & Speech Targets (Ages 5–6)
**Curriculum Guide**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Language Guide / Reading & Literacy
**Key Stage**: Key Stage 1 (Early Literacy & Word Study)

---

## 1. DepEd MATATAG Competencies
* **RL1PA-I-1**: Chant nursery rhymes and poems (L, S).
* **RL1PA-I-2**: Segment a two-three syllable word into its syllabic parts (L, S).
* **RL1PA-I-3**: Identify rhyming words in nursery rhymes, poems, and chants (L, S).
* **RL1PA-I-4**: Say two or three words that rhyme (L, S).
* **RL1PA-I-5**: Identify initial sounds (vowels, consonants, semi-vowels) (L, S).
* **RL1PWS-I-1**: Produce the sound of the letters of L1 (L, S, R).
* **RL1PWS-I-2**: Identify the letters in L1 (L, S, R).
* **RL1PWS-I-3**: Isolate sounds (consonants and vowels) in a word (beginning and ending) (L, S, R).
* **RL1PWS-I-4**: Substitute individual sounds in simple words to make new words (L, S, R).
* **RL1PWS-I-5**: Sound out words accurately (L, S, R).

---

## 2. Speech & Phonological Focus
* **ASHA Targets**: Liquids `/l, r/`, affricates `/tʃ, dʒ/`, fricatives `/f, v, s, ʃ, θ/`, S/L/R consonant clusters.
* **Clinical Focus**: Progression from sound isolation to CVC words, carrier phrases, and simple complete sentences.

---
