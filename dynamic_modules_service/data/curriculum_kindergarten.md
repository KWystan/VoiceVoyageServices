# Kindergarten Curriculum & Speech Targets (Ages 4–5)
**Curriculum Guide**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Curriculum Guide
**Key Stage**: Key Stage 1 (Foundational Literacy)

---

## 1. DepEd MATATAG Competencies
* **Competency 7**: Identify one's given name, friends' names, family members, and common household items.
* **Competency 8**: Produce the sound of the letter it stands for.
* **Competency 15**: Use communication tools properly and technology appropriately.
* **Competency 23**: Recognize that sounding off letters forms words.

---

## 2. Speech & Phonological Focus
* **ASHA Targets**: Early stops and nasals (`/p, b, m, n, t, d/`), Initial Consonant Deletion (ICD), Final Consonant Deletion (FCD), Weak Syllable Deletion (WSD).
* **Clinical Focus**: Phonetic motor placement, single-syllable CVC words, high visual cue support, auditory discrimination.

---

## 3. Island Gameplay Maps (Kindergarten / Ages 4–5)

### Island 1: "My School & My World" (Weeks 1–2)
* **MATATAG Focus**: Self-Identity, Names, Colors, Shapes, Spatial Positions
* **Target Processes**: Early stops/nasals (/p, b, m, n/), ICD, FCD, WSD

| Level | MATATAG Focus | Speech Target | 5-Stage Mechanic | Interaction & Dynamic Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1.1 Name & Letter Station** | Name spelling, letter recognition | Initial consonants (/p, b, m, n/), ICD | **Step 1 - Picture Listen** | Taps 3 familiar images. *Targets*: Pig, Ball, Map, Pen, Book. |
| **1.2 Classroom Bag Collector** | Classroom routines, color sorting | Weak Syllable Deletion (WSD) | **Step 2 - Sound Bucket** | Drags spoken bubble to matching bucket. *Targets*: Banana, Elephant, Paper, Pencil. |
| **1.3 Sound Beach Clean-Up** | Object classification, spatial concepts | Final Consonant Deletion (FCD) | **Step 2 - Find the Sound** | Selects item ending with target sound. *Targets*: Cup, Sun, Boat, Bed, Bike, Bag. |
| **1.4 Syllable Bridge Crossing** | Number sense 1-3, step counting | Multisyllabic stops (/t, d/) | **Step 3 - Guided Training Path** | Repeats word after model to cross obstacles. *Targets*: Ten, Dog, Water, Ladder. |

### Island 2: "My Body & My Feelings" (Weeks 3–6)
* **MATATAG Focus**: Emotions, Body Parts, 5 Senses, Hygiene, Numbers 1–5
* **Target Processes**: Fronting (/k, ɡ/ -> /t, d/), Stopping of fricatives (/s, f/ -> /t, p/), Nasal /m/

| Level | MATATAG Focus | Speech Target | 5-Stage Mechanic | Interaction & Dynamic Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2.1 Emotion Mirror Lagoon** | Identifying basic feelings | Stopping (/s, f/ -> /t, p/) | **Step 2 - Find the Word** | Selects target emotion from 4 choices. *Targets*: Sun, Sad, Face, Smile, Happy. |
| **2.2 Body Parts Puzzle** | Identifying body parts | Fronting (/k, ɡ/ -> /t, d/) | **Step 3 - Build & Say** | Completes letter tile, hears model, repeats. *Targets*: Key, Goat, Coat, Cup. |
| **2.3 Senses & Hygiene Trail** | Hygiene habits, 5 senses | Fricative /f/, nasal /m/ | **Step 3 - Listen, Pop & Repeat** | Pops hygiene bubble, repeats into mic. *Targets*: Fish, Muffin, Leaf, Mop, Soap. |
| **2.4 Echo Cave (Voice Studio)** | Verbal self-expression | Auditory feedback & phrases | **Step 5 - Talk With Buddy** | Answers mascot with target phrase. *Targets*: 'See the sun', 'Clean my coat'. |

### Island 3: "Family & Healthy Food" (Weeks 7–8)
* **MATATAG Focus**: Family Roles, Healthy Foods (Go, Grow, Glow)
* **Target Processes**: Gliding (/j, h/, /r, l/ -> /w, j/), Palatal Fronting (/ʃ/ -> /s/), Cluster Reduction (S/L blends)

| Level | MATATAG Focus | Speech Target | 5-Stage Mechanic | Interaction & Dynamic Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3.1 Family Tree Sorting** | Family roles and relationships | Gliding (/l, r/ -> /w, j/) | **Step 2 - Sound Bucket** | Matches family word bubbles to buckets. *Targets*: Father, Mother, Baby, House. |
| **3.2 Healthy Plate Market** | Food groups (Go, Grow, Glow) | Palatal Fronting (/ʃ/ -> /s/) | **Step 4 - Forest Discovery** | Finds hidden market item and names it independently. *Targets*: Shoe, Fish, Shell, Shake. |
| **3.3 Cluster Garden** | Environmental objects, counting 1-5 | Cluster Reduction (S/L blends) | **Step 3 - Build & Say** | Assembles blend tile (`S + TAR -> STAR`), repeats model. *Targets*: Star, Spider, Plane, Blue. |
| **3.4 Sound Path Jumper** | Ordinals, counting 1-5 | Consonant blend synthesis | **Step 4 - Build the Bridge** | Says blend word independently to build bridge. *Targets*: Spider, Star, Blue, Plane, Spoon. |

### Island 4: "Home & Story Land" (Weeks 9–10)
* **MATATAG Focus**: Calendar, Days of Week, Story Sequencing (3 Little Pigs), Numbers 0–10
* **Target Processes**: Stopping review, Multisyllabic phrases, Minimal pair discrimination

| Level | MATATAG Focus | Speech Target | 5-Stage Mechanic | Interaction & Dynamic Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4.1 Calendar Castle** | Calendar dates, counting 0-10 | Voiced/voiceless stops & /s, z/ | **Step 2 - Find the Sound** | Selects calendar item with target sound. *Targets*: Sun, Seven, Six, Zebra, Bus. |
| **4.2 Story Sequencer** | Story comprehension (3 Pigs) | Multisyllabic sentence phrases | **Step 5 - Story Adventure** | Spoken answers advance story scenes. *Targets*: Pig, Straw, House, Blow, Brick. |
| **4.3 Rhyme House Match** | Auditory rhyming awareness | Minimal-pair discrimination | **Step 2 - Find the Word** | Distinguishes contrast pairs. *Targets*: Key-Tea, Sun-Tun, Boat-Coat, Pig-Big. |
| **4.4 Sound Explorer Gate** | Q1 synthesis & cumulative review | Target phoneme integration | **Step 5 - Mission Roleplay** | Completes communication mission passwords. *Targets*: 'My school bag', 'I love family'. |

### Island 5: "Mastery Island" (Post-Assessment)
* **Purpose**: Stress-free interactive post-assessment evaluating speech progress.

| Level | Diagnostic Screener Objective | Speech Target | 5-Stage Mechanic | Assessment Target Items |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5.1 Echo Waterfall** | Re-evaluates early stops, ICD, FCD | Early stops, nasals, ICD, FCD | **Step 4 - Forest Discovery** | `Pig`, `Ball`, `Cup`, `Sun`, `Ten`, `Bed`. |
| **5.2 Magic Mirror** | Re-evaluates velar vs. alveolar | Fronting & Backing | **Step 4 - Silly Monster** | `Key`, `Goat`, `Cookie`, `Tiger`, `Bike`, `Bag`. |
| **5.3 Fricative & Cluster Bridge** | Re-evaluates Age 5 fricatives & blends | Stopping & Cluster Reduction | **Step 4 - Build the Bridge** | `Sun`, `Bus`, `Fish`, `Shoe`, `Spider`, `Star`, `Blue`, `Plane`. |
| **5.4 Grand Story Ceremony** | Evaluates overall speech intelligibility | Connected speech fluency | **Step 5 - Story Adventure** | 1. *"I go to school."* 2. *"I see my family."* 3. *"I play on the island."* |
