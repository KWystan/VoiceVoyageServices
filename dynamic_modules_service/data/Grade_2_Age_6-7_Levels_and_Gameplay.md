# Grade 2 (Age 6–7) — Levels and Gameplay

Continuation of the Kindergarten format in FINAL DEFENSE DELIVERABLES.pdf

**Basis:** MATATAG-Curriculum-Grade-2-10-English.pdf (pp. 47–60) and Age 6–7 ASHA-Aligned Speech Sound Development Word List for Pre-Assessment.pdf. The ASHA-aligned words are treated as sample assessment targets rather than a fixed word bank. **Design principle:** Grade 2 progresses from word-level speech production toward phrases, sentences, and connected speech, while aligning each gameplay level with Grade 2 MATATAG competencies.

## Island 1: "My Family Adventure" (Quarter 1)

**MATATAG Focus:** Myself & My Family, personal information, family relationships, everyday experiences, rhymes, onset-rime, CVC words, sight words, simple sentences, story elements.

**ASHA Target (Age 6–7):** Late-developing liquids and fricatives — /l/, /r/, /v/; reducing Gliding and Stopping.

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1: Family Portrait Gallery | Identify family members; use vocabulary referring to oneself and family. | /l/, /r/ — Gliding reduction. | BucketSortWidget (Family Sort): Drag family members into correct family groups while saying each target word aloud. | Default: mother, father, sister, brother, baby. Error Target: dynamically increases words containing the child's /l/ or /r/ target, e.g., Lion, Balloon, Bell, Rock, Mirror, Star. |
| 1.2: Rhyme River | Recognize rhymes in chants, poems, and stories; segment onset and rime. | /l/, /r/ in initial, medial, and final positions; Gliding. | MinimalPairWidget (Rhyme & Sound Match): Match rhyming picture cards, then repeat the selected target word. | Default: rhyming pairs from the current lesson. Error Target: pairs are selected to contain the child's weak /l/ or /r/ sound. |
| 1.3: Family Word Hunt | Identify sight words and read CVC words accurately. | /v/ — Stopping; /l/, /r/ — Gliding. | TapAndPopWidget (Word Hunt): Tap floating family-related words and pronounce them to collect them. | Default: Grade 2 sight words and family vocabulary. Error Target: increased /v/, /l/, or /r/ targets; sample /v/ words include Volcano, Seven, Glove. |
| 1.4: Family Story Path | Sequence events; identify characters, setting, problem/solution; relate events to one's experience. | Connected production of /l/, /r/, /v/ targets; Gliding/Stopping review. | SequenceJumperWidget (Story Path): Jump across 3–4 story events in chronological order while narrating each event. | Default: 3-event family story. Error Target: story vocabulary is dynamically regenerated to contain the child's target phonemes. |

## Island 2: "School Quest" (Quarter 2)

**MATATAG Focus:** School, classroom experiences, high-frequency and content-specific vocabulary, CVC/CVCe/CVVC patterns, simple sentences, personal experiences.

**ASHA Target (Age 6–7):** /l/, /r/, /v/ with emphasis on reducing Gliding and Stopping.

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1: Classroom Collector | Use vocabulary referring to school; identify nouns, verbs, and adjectives. | /l/, /r/ — Gliding. | BucketSortWidget (Classroom Sort): Sort school objects into classroom categories while naming each object. | Default: book, pencil, paper, teacher, desk, bag. Error Target: prioritizes words containing the child's /l/ or /r/ target. |
| 2.2: Sound Locker | Read CVC, CVCe, and CVVC words accurately and automatically. | /v/ — Stopping; /l/, /r/ — Gliding. | TapAndPopWidget (Locker Pop): Tap school objects to reveal target words and pronounce them. | Default: grade-level word patterns. Error Target: AI selects words matching the child's weak phoneme and required word pattern. |
| 2.3: Speak & Build | Use simple sentences about school with proper intonation and sequence words to convey meaning. | /l/, /r/, /v/ in phrases/sentences; Gliding and Stopping. | EchoCaveWidget (Speak & Build): Child records a sentence and hears playback alongside the model. | Default: "I go to school." / "I have a new book." Error Target: sentence generator inserts target phonemes into the same grammatical structure. |
| 2.4: School Story Challenge | Read grade-level sentences with appropriate speed, accuracy, and expression; comprehend and summarize stories. | Connected speech containing /l/, /r/, /v/. | SequenceJumperWidget (Story Challenge): Complete 3–4 story checkpoints by saying a sentence at each checkpoint. | Default: short school story. Error Target: story sentences are regenerated around the child's target sounds while retaining Grade 2 vocabulary and sentence complexity. |

## Island 3: "Community Explorer" (Quarter 3)

**MATATAG Focus:** Community, community vocabulary, CVC/CVCe/CVVC/CVCC/CCVC words, sentence types, story sequencing, cause/effect, and description.

**ASHA Target (Age 6–7):** /ch/, /j/ — Deaffrication; R-clusters — Cluster Reduction; continued /l/, /r/ mastery.

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1: Community Helper Hunt | Use community vocabulary; identify nouns, verbs, adjectives, and pronouns. | /ch/, /j/ — Deaffrication. | TapAndPopWidget (Helper Hunt): Tap community helpers and objects appearing around the town. | Default: teacher, doctor, police officer, shop, jeep, chair. Error Target: increases /ch/ and /j/ words; sample targets include Chair, Jeep, Kitchen, Orange, Watch, Bridge. |
| 3.2: Cluster Construction Site | Read CCVC words and consonant clusters accurately. | R-clusters — Cluster Reduction. | SequenceJumperWidget (Build the Bridge): Each correctly pronounced cluster word places another bridge piece. | Default: Grade 2 CCVC vocabulary. Error Target: prioritizes R-clusters; sample targets include Frog, Truck. |
| 3.3: Sound Match Market | Identify words with different functions; use community vocabulary and content-specific words. | /ch/, /j/, /r/ — Deaffrication/Gliding. | MinimalPairWidget (Market Match): Match picture cards and pronounce the contrasting words. | Default: community-related vocabulary. Error Target: creates contrastive pairs around the child's detected error. |
| 3.4: Community Story Road | Sequence 3–4 events; identify problem/solution, cause/effect, and predict an ending. | R-clusters + /ch/, /j/, /r/ in connected speech. | EchoCaveWidget (Story Road): Speak one sentence at each story checkpoint to move forward. | Default: short community story. Error Target: target phonemes are embedded repeatedly into story vocabulary and sentences. |

## Island 4: "Environment Expedition" (Quarter 4)

**MATATAG Focus:** Physical environment, Science and Mathematics vocabulary, CVCe/CVVC/CVCC/CCVC/VCV/VCCV words, synonyms/antonyms, sentence types, descriptions, procedural and time-order texts.

**ASHA Target (Age 6–7):** Integrated mastery of /l/, /r/, /v/, /ch/, /j/ and R-clusters, with reduced Gliding, Stopping, Deaffrication, and Cluster Reduction.

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection (Default vs. Error Target) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1: Nature Sound Safari | Use vocabulary referring to the physical environment; use Science vocabulary. | /l/, /r/, /v/ — Gliding and Stopping review. | TapAndPopWidget (Sound Safari): Tap animals, plants, and environmental objects while producing their names. | Default: environment/Science vocabulary. Error Target: prioritizes the child's weakest liquid/fricative. |
| 4.2: Explorer's Word Trail | Read CVCe, CVVC, CVCC, CCVC, VCV, and VCCV patterns. | R-clusters + /ch/, /j/; Cluster Reduction and Deaffrication. | SequenceJumperWidget (Word Trail): Pronounce a target word to unlock the next trail tile. | Default: Grade 2 word-pattern vocabulary. Error Target: selects word patterns containing the child's target phoneme/process. |
| 4.3: Environment Builder | Use declarative, interrogative, imperative, and exclamatory sentences; use proper punctuation and intonation. | All Age 6–7 targets in sentence production. | EchoCaveWidget (Build-a-Sentence): Arrange visual word tiles, then speak the completed sentence into the microphone. | Default: "The frog is near the tree." Error Target: AI restructures sentences so the target phoneme occurs naturally multiple times without changing the intended Grade 2 grammar competency. |
| 4.4: Grand Explorer Challenge | Comprehend stories/informational texts, identify details, sequence events, summarize, and express ideas using time-order/procedural text. | Cumulative mastery: /l/, /r/, /v/, /ch/, /j/, R-clusters. | EchoCaveWidget + SequenceJumperWidget (Final Expedition): Complete a 3–4 step mission, pronounce target words, then narrate a short procedural sequence. | Default: environment-based procedural task. Error Target: dynamically inserts unresolved phonemes into mission vocabulary and narration. |

## Grade 2 Speech-Sound Progression

| Detected Error | Grade 2 Target | Gameplay Adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Gliding | /l/, /r/ | Increase words containing /l/ or /r/ and gradually progress word → phrase → sentence → connected speech. |
| Stopping | /v/ | Increase /v/ targets and contrast the child's production with the correct model. |
| Deaffrication | /ch/, /j/ | Increase /ch/ and /j/ targets using picture matching and repeated production. |
| Cluster Reduction | R-clusters | Increase CCVC words with R-clusters and require accurate cluster production. |
| No significant error | Mixed /l, r, v, ch, j/ + R-clusters | Move from isolated words into phrases, sentences, and narrative tasks. |

## Design Notes for Defense

- Grade 2 should not simply repeat the Kindergarten speech targets. The progression should visibly move toward late-developing liquids/fricatives, affricates, clusters, and connected-speech production.
- The Age 6–7 ASHA-aligned file supplies sample assessment words. These should not become the system's fixed word bank.
- The dynamic content system should generate additional age- and curriculum-appropriate words based on the detected phoneme/process and the current MATATAG competency.
- Suggested overall progression: /l/ + /r/ → /v/ → /ch/ + /j/ → R-clusters → connected-speech mastery.
