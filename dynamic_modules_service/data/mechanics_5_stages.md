# Voice Voyage: 5-Stage Speech-Learning Progression & 15 Gameplay Mechanics
**Pedagogical Framework**: Hierarchical cueing moving from auditory familiarization to spontaneous contextual communication.

---

## 1. Stage Progression Hierarchy

| Stage | Main Goal | Speech Demand | Mechanics Included |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Step 1 - Listen** | Auditory exposure and familiarization with target sounds. | **No speaking required** | 1, 2, 3 |
| **Step 2 - Recognize** | Receptive discrimination and sound-position localization. | **Listening/selection only** | 4, 5, 6 |
| **Step 3 - Guided Say** | Immediate modeled imitation and motor placement practice. | **Speaking required (Modeled)** | 7, 8, 9 |
| **Step 4 - Independent Say** | Spontaneous word retrieval without immediate audio model. | **Independent speaking required** | 10, 11, 12 |
| **Step 5 - Use in Context** | Generalization into carrier phrases, dialogues, and stories. | **Contextual speech required** | 13, 14, 15 |

---

## 2. The 15 Concrete Gameplay Mechanics

### Step 1: Listen (Auditory Exposure)
1. **Picture Listen**: 3 target pictures appear. Tapping plays target word and gives visual feedback. (Exposure only).
2. **Sound Emphasis**: Visual/acoustic stretching or highlighting of target sound in word position (e.g., *"ssssun"*, *"kkkkey"*).
3. **Falling Sound Bubbles**: Floating bubbles fall down. Tapping pops bubble, reveals picture, and plays audio.

### Step 2: Recognize (Receptive Discrimination)
4. **Find the Word**: Receptive auditory question (*"Which one is the key?"*) with 4 image choices.
5. **Find the Sound**: Sound location task (*"Find the word that starts with /k/"* or *"ends with /g/"*).
6. **Sound Bucket**: Dragging audio bubbles into matching categorized image buckets.

### Step 3: Guided Say (Modeled Speech Production)
7. **Build & Say**: Missing letter/chunk tile assembly (`DO _ + G -> DOG`) -> audio model -> prompt *"Can you say dog?"* -> mic records.
8. **Guided Training Path**: Obstacle path course. Hearing model word and repeating it allows character to jump obstacle.
9. **Listen, Pop & Repeat**: Pop bubble -> audio model plays -> *"Your turn"* -> speech recorded.

### Step 4: Independent Say (Spontaneous Speech Production)
10. **Forest Discovery**: Exploration under bushes/rocks reveals hidden objects. App asks *"What did you find?"* without prior audio model.
11. **Silly Monster**: Monster intentionally mispronounces word (says *"tey"* for *key*); child says *"key"* to fill friendship meter.
12. **Build the Bridge**: Child independently names displayed word bubbles to construct bridge planks for character to cross.

### Step 5: Use in Context (Generalization & Discourse)
13. **Talk With Buddy**: Controlled dialogue graph with mascot (*"What is this?"* -> *"Cat"* -> *"What color is the cat?"* -> *"The cat is black"*).
14. **Mission Roleplay**: Practical scenarios (e.g., store mission: *"Ask the shopkeeper for a carrot"* -> *"Can I have a carrot?"*).
15. **Story Adventure**: Branching narrative sequence where contextual spoken responses unlock subsequent scenes.
