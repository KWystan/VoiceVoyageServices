# Grade 3 Curriculum & Speech Targets (Age 8)
**Curriculum Guide**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 3 Curriculum Guide (pp. 61–76)
**Key Stage**: Key Stage 1 (Advanced Word Study & Informational Discourse)

---

## 1. DepEd MATATAG Competencies
* **EN3PWS-I-1**: Identify Grade 3 level-appropriate sight words (L, S, R, W).
* **EN3PWS-I-2**: Read words accurately and automatically according to word patterns (initial, final, medial):
  1. **CVCC words** (L, S, R, W)
  2. **CCVC words** (clusters and diphthongs) (L, S, R, W)
  3. **VCV words** (L, S, R, W)
  4. **VCCV words** (multisyllabic words) (L, S, R, W)

---

## 2. Speech & Phonological Focus
* **ASHA Targets**: Voiceless interdental `/θ/` ("th", *think, bath*), voiced interdental `/ð/` (*this, weather*), voiced palatal fricative `/ʒ/` (*treasure, vision*), 3-4 syllable multisyllabic words.
* **Clinical Focus**: Weak syllable deletion resolution, complex sentence formation, informational narration, conversational articulation clarity.

---
