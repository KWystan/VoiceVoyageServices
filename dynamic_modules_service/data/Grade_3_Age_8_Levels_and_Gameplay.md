# Grade 3 (Age 8) — Levels and Gameplay

Continuation of the Kindergarten format in FINAL DEFENSE DELIVERABLES.pdf

**Basis:** MATATAG-Curriculum-Grade-2-10-English.pdf (pp. 61–76) and Age 8 ASHA-Aligned Speech Sound Development Word List for Pre-Assessment.pdf.

**Design principle:** Grade 3 progresses toward complex integration, /th/ mastery, /zh/, multisyllabic complexity, and fluent connected speech. The ASHA-aligned words are sample assessment targets, not a fixed word bank.

## Island 1: "Regional Discovery" — Quarter 1

**MATATAG Focus:** Regional themes, high-frequency and content-specific vocabulary, CVCC/CCVC/VCV/VCCV words, sentence types, story comprehension, and retelling.

**ASHA Target (Age 8):** /th/ voiceless and voiced, /zh/, multisyllabic complexity; reduction of Stopping, Initial/Final Consonant Deletion, and Weak Syllable/Syllable Reduction where applicable.

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1: Regional Word Explorer | Identify Grade 3 sight words; read CVCC, CCVC, VCV, and VCCV words. | /th/ and /zh/; Stopping and deletion review. | TapAndPopWidget (Word Explorer): Tap floating regional objects and pronounce the displayed word to collect them. | Default: Grade 3 sight words and regional vocabulary. Error Target: AI increases words containing the child's unresolved /th/ or /zh/ sound. |
| 1.2: Sound Contrast Canyon | Identify vocabulary and distinguish words according to meaning and function. | Voiced /th/ vs. voiceless /th/; Stopping. | MinimalPairWidget (Sound Match): Match audio/picture cards based on the target sound. | Default: mixed Grade 3 vocabulary. Error Target: dynamically generates contrasts emphasizing the child's incorrect /th/ production. |
| 1.3: Multisyllable Mountain | Read and use words accurately according to word patterns; identify roots of high-frequency words. | Multisyllabic complexity; Weak Syllable/Syllable Reduction. | SequenceJumperWidget (Syllable Climb): Child climbs one platform per correctly produced syllable. | Default: 2–4 syllable Grade 3 words. Error Target: increases multisyllabic words where the child omits or reduces syllables. |
| 1.4: Regional Story Quest | Sequence 4–5 events; identify problem/solution, character traits, cause/effect, predict endings, and summarize. | /th/, /zh/ and multisyllabic words in connected speech. | EchoCaveWidget + SequenceJumperWidget (Story Quest): Arrange events then narrate the completed sequence. | Default: regional-themed story. Error Target: AI rewrites story vocabulary/sentences to naturally contain the child's target sounds. |

## Island 2: "Science & Math Expedition" — Quarter 2

**MATATAG Focus:** Regional and content-specific topics, Mathematics and Science vocabulary, synonyms/antonyms, word roots, simple and compound sentences.

**ASHA Target (Age 8):** /th/, /zh/ and multisyllabic words in increasingly complex phrases and sentences.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1: Science Lab Hunt | Use Science and Mathematics content-specific vocabulary. | /th/, /zh/; Stopping review. | BucketSortWidget (Science Sort): Sort objects into Science/Math categories while saying each word. | Default: age-appropriate Science and Math terms. Error Target: prioritizes terms containing the child's target phoneme. |
| 2.2: Word Root Reactor | Identify roots of high-frequency nouns, verbs, and adjectives; identify synonyms and antonyms. | Multisyllabic production; Syllable Reduction. | TapAndPopWidget (Root Reactor): Tap words to reveal their root and pronounce both forms. | Default: common Grade 3 roots and word families. Error Target: selects longer words containing unresolved syllable or phoneme patterns. |

Error Target: AI inserts target phonemes while maintaining the required grammatical structure. |
| 2.4: Compound Sentence Bridge | Identify two independent clauses and the coordinating conjunction in compound sentences; use correct punctuation. | Connected /th/, /zh/ and multisyllabic vocabulary. | SequenceJumperWidget (Clause Bridge): Each correctly spoken clause builds another section of a bridge; child connects both clauses. | Default: age-appropriate compound sentences. Error Target: generated clauses contain repeated but natural target sounds. |

**MATATAG Focus:** National themes, Science/Mathematics vocabulary, demonstrative pronouns, compound sentences, five-event stories, and informational texts.

**ASHA Target (Age 8):** Integrated /th/, /zh/ and multisyllabic production during longer phrases, sentences, and narrative speech.

| Level & Name | DepEd MATATAG Focus | Phonological Target & Process | Flutter UI Template & Mechanics | Dynamic Content Injection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1: Hero Vocabulary Hunt | Use vocabulary relating to national themes and content-specific topics. | /th/, /zh/. | TapAndPopWidget (Hero Hunt): Tap historically themed objects/characters and pronounce their names. | Default: national-theme vocabulary. | 3.2: Meaning Match Arena | Identify synonyms, antonyms, verbs, adjectives, and demonstrative pronouns. | /th/ voiced/voiceless contrast; /zh/. | MinimalPairWidget (Meaning Match): Match words with synonyms/antonyms while verbally producing the selected word. | Default: Grade 3 vocabulary. | 3.4: National Story Studio | Retell myths, legends, fables, and narrative poems using one's own words. | Connected-speech fluency; multisyllabic integration. | EchoCaveWidget (Story Studio): Listen to a short story, then record a retelling using visual story prompts. | Default: age-appropriate Philippine-themed narrative. **MATATAG Focus:** National themes, content-specific vocabulary, simple and compound sentences, informational texts, procedural/descriptive/explanatory texts, and summary.

**ASHA Target (Age 8):** Complex integration — /th/ voiced/voiceless, /zh/, multisyllabic words, and connected narrative speech.

## Grade 3 / Age 8 Speech-Sound Progression

| Screening Result | Grade 3 Target | Adaptive Gameplay |
|---|---|---|
| Stopping of /th/ | Voiceless /th/ and voiced /th/ | Increase /th/ words, then phrases and sentences; provide auditory/model feedback. |
| Initial Consonant Deletion | Initial consonants, especially /th/ | Increase initial-position target words and use visual highlighting of the missing sound. |
| Final Consonant Deletion | Final /th/ and other word-final consonants | Increase final-position words such as sample mouth/smooth targets. |
| Syllable Reduction | Multisyllabic words | Increase 3–5 syllable words and use syllable-by-syllable gameplay. |
| /zh/ substitution/stopping | /zh/ | Increase words containing /zh/, especially medial-position words such as the sample treasure target. |
| No significant error | Complex integration | Move from words → phrases → sentences → narrative/informational speech. |

## Recommended Grade 3 Difficulty Progression

**Grade 2:** /l/, /r/, /v/, /ch/, /j/ + R-clusters → word → phrase → sentence → connected speech.

**Grade 3 / Age 8:** /th/ + /zh/ + multisyllabic complexity → word → multisyllabic word → phrase → simple sentence → compound sentence → narrative/informational speech.

## Defense Note

The Age 8 reference provides sample assessment targets such as Thumb, Toothbrush, Mouth, They, Feather, Smooth, Treasure, Helicopter, Vegetable, and Spaghetti. These should not be presented as the complete Grade 3 word list. They are seed/reference examples for the dynamic content system.

**Recommended AI adaptation:** Detected phoneme/process → word position → syllable complexity → MATATAG competency → gameplay level.
