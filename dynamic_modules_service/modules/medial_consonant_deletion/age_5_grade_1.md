# Learning Module: Medial Consonant Deletion (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `mcd-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Medial Consonant Deletion (MCD: VCV -> VV)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PA-I-2`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.1 Sound Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PA-I-2` (Syllable segmentation with medial consonants).
- **How It Works**: Three Grade 1 images appear:
  1. **Rabbit** (Medial /b/) — `/ɹæb.ɪt/`
  2. **Ladder** (Medial /d/) — `/læd.ɚ/`
  3. **Chicken** (Medial /k/) — `/tʃɪk.ən/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to reinforce middle consonant articulation.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Audio modeling of medial stops and fricatives.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Syllable Porch** — Clapping multi-syllabic words with distinct medial beats.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Community Walk** — Guided imitation of 2-syllable items.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I climb the la-er"* -> Child fixes: *"ladder!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"The rabbit hops fast."*, *"My father climbs the ladder."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `rab`, `bit`, `lad`, `der`
- **Words**: `rabbit`, `ladder`, `chicken`, `paper`, `water`
- **Phrases**: `a white rabbit`, `a tall ladder`, `a little chicken`
- **Sentences**: `The white rabbit hops.`, `I see a tall ladder.`, `The little chicken eats.`\n
