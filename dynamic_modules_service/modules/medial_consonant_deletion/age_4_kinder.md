# Learning Module: Medial Consonant Deletion (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `mcd-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Medial Consonant Deletion (MCD: VCV -> VV)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 15)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.4 Syllable Bridge Crossing)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competency 15 (Syllable connection across medial consonants).
- **How It Works**: Three multisyllabic images appear:
  1. **Paper** (Medial /p/) — `/peɪ.pɚ/`
  2. **Water** (Medial /t/) — `/wɔ.tɚ/`
  3. **Puppy** (Medial /p/) — `/pʌp.i/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear the strong middle consonant bridge (*"pa-P-er! NOT pa-er!"*, *"wa-T-er! NOT wa-er!"*).

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of medial closure.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Classroom Bag Collector** — Identifying words with complete middle consonants.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Syllable Bridge Crossing** — Step-by-step repetition: `pa` -> `per` -> `paper`.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I need pa-er"* -> Child fixes: *"paper!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I write on white paper."*, *"I drink cold water."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `pa`, `per`, `wa`, `ter`
- **Words**: `paper`, `water`, `puppy`, `baby`, `cookie`
- **Phrases**: `white paper`, `cold water`, `a little puppy`
