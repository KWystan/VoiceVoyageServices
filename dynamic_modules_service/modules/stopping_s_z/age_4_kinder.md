# Learning Module: Stopping of /s, z/ (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `stopping-sz-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/s, z/ -> [t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (90% Suppression Milestone ~4;6)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 15, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 2: *"My Body & My Feelings"* (Level 2.1 Emotion Mirror Lagoon)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework

### 1.1 MATATAG Curriculum Focus
- **Competency 7 & 8**: Letter sounds /s/ and /z/, identifying familiar words and emotion terms.
- **Competency 23**: Recognizing letter-sound blending in CVC words.
- **Thematic Context**: Body parts, feelings, smiles, and daily kindergarten routines.

### 1.2 How It Works (Level 1.1 Mechanics)
- **Visual Display**: Three familiar images with starting letters appear:
  1. **Sun** (Letter **S**) — `/sʌn/`
  2. **Sock** (Letter **S**) — `/sɑk/`
  3. **Zebra** (Letter **Z**) — `/zi.bɹə/`
- **Interaction**: The child taps all three images/letters.
- **Audio Feedback**: Tapping plays the continuous friction sound with acoustic stretching and glowing visual airflow streams:
  - *"S says /s/... sss-sun! Sun!"*
  - *"S says /s/... sss-sock! Sock!"*
  - *"Z says /z/... zzz-zebra! Zebra!"*
- **Speech Goal**: Contrast continuous friction airflow against premature stop closure (*[tʌn]* "tun" -> *[sʌn]* "sun").

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Speech-Learning Progression

- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Taps 3 images (*Sun*, *Sock*, *Zebra*) to hear continuous hissing models. (No speaking required).
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **2.1 Emotion Mirror Lagoon / Sound Bucket** — Sorts words that hiss (/s, z/) vs words that pop (/t, d/).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **2.2 Build & Say** — Assembles tile `S + UN -> SUN`. Modeled prompt: *"Can you say sun with a long sss sound?"*. Child repeats into mic.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **2.3 Silly Monster** — Silly monster says *"Look at the tun!"*. Child says *"sun"* independently to correct monster.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **2.4 Echo Cave & Talk With Buddy** — Mascot asks: *"What is in the sky?"* -> Child responds: *"I see the sun."*
