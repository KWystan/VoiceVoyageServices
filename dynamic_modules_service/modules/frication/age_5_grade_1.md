# Learning Module: Frication (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `frication-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Frication (Stop/Nasal -> Fricative)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.1 Sound Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-1` (Clean stop letter sounds).
- **How It Works**: Three stop images appear:
  1. **Two** (Stop /t/) — `/tu/`
  2. **Cup** (Stop /p/) — `/kʌp/`
  3. **Ten** (Stop /t/) — `/tɛn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to practice clean stop releases.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Acoustic discrimination of stop pop vs fricative hiss.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Rhyme Porch** — Identifying stop codas.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Community Walk** — Guided path modeled imitation.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I drink from a cuff"* -> Child fixes: *"cup!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"We have two clean cups on the table."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `ta`, `to`, `pa`, `ba`
- **Words**: `two`, `ten`, `cup`, `top`, `bed`, `pig`, `cat`
- **Phrases**: `two blue cups`, `a fast top`, `ten green apples`
- **Sentences**: `I place two blue cups on the table.`, `The fast top spins.`, `I eat ten green apples.`\n
