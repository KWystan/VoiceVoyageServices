# Learning Module: Frication (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `frication-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Frication (Stop/Nasal -> Fricative, e.g. /t, p, b/ -> [s, f, v])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 8, 23 (Crisp stop closure /t, p, b/ vs hissing friction).
- **How It Works**: Three stop images appear:
  1. **Two** (Stop /t/) — `/tu/`
  2. **Cup** (Stop /p/ coda) — `/kʌp/`
  3. **Ten** (Stop /t/) — `/tɛn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear clean stop pops (*"t-t-two! NOT soo!"*, *"c-u-P! NOT c-u-F!"*).

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of complete stop closure and release.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Bucket** — Sorting popping stops (*two*, *cup*, *ten*) from hissing sounds (*sun*, *fish*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Building `T + EN -> TEN`. Prompt: *"Tap your tongue quickly like a pop: t-ten"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I want soo cookies"* -> Child fixes: *"two!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I have two red cups."*, *"I count ten books."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `ta`, `tee`, `pa`, `ba`
- **Words**: `two`, `ten`, `cup`, `top`, `bed`, `pig`
- **Phrases**: `two red cups`, `ten big books`, `a little pig`
