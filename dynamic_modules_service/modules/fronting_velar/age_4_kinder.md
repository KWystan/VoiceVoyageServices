# Learning Module: Velar Fronting (/k, ɡ/) (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `fronting-velar-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Velar Fronting (/k, ɡ/ -> [t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Resolving ~3;6–4;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 15, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 2: *"My Body & My Feelings"* (Level 2.2 Body Parts Puzzle)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 7, 8 (Letters K, G; back-of-the-mouth tongue contact).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Car** (Letter **C/K**) — `/kɑɹ/`
  2. **Goat** (Letter **G**) — `/ɡoʊt/`
  3. **Cup** (Letter **C**) — `/kʌp/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear the deep throat-click sound: *"K says /k/... k-k-car! Car!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Visual animation showing tongue pulling back against the soft palate.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **2.2 Sound Bucket** — Sorting back sounds (*car*, *goat*, *cup*) from front sounds (*tea*, *door*, *tub*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **2.2 Build & Say** — Assembling `C + UP -> CUP`. Model prompt: *"Open your mouth wide and push the sound from the back: k-cup"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **2.3 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I want a tup"* -> Child fixes: *"cup!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **2.4 Echo Cave** — *"I ride in the car."*, *"The goat is on the grass."*

---
