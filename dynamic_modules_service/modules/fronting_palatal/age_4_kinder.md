# Learning Module: Palatal Fronting (/ʃ/ -> [s]) (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `fronting-palatal-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Palatal Fronting (/ʃ/ -> [s])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~4;6–5;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 3: *"Family & Healthy Food"* (Level 3.2 Healthy Plate Market)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 8, 23 (Mid-palate tongue posture vs front teeth contact).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Shoe** (Letters **SH**) — `/ʃu/`
  2. **Fish** (Letters **SH**) — `/fɪʃ/`
  3. **Shell** (Letters **SH**) — `/ʃɛl/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear mid-palatal friction: *"SH says /ʃ/ (shhh)! sh-shoe! Shoe!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of round lips and retracted tongue body.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.2 Market Sort** — Distinguishing front /s/ (*sun*, *soap*) from mid-palatal /ʃ/ (*shoe*, *shell*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.2 Build & Say** — Modeled imitation with visual lip-rounding cues.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.3 Forest Discovery** — Independent naming of hidden shells.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.4 Talk With Buddy** — *"I have a new shoe."*, *"I see a little fish."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `sha`, `shee`, `sho`, `shu`
- **Words**: `shoe`, `fish`, `shell`, `ship`, `sheep`
- **Phrases**: `a blue shoe`, `a swimming fish`, `a shiny shell`
