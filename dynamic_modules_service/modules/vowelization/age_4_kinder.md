# Learning Module: Vowelization of Post-Vocalic Liquids (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `vowelization-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Vowelization (/l, r/ coda -> [o, u, ə])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~5;0–6;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competency 8 (Coda sounds L/R in familiar toys).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Ball** (Coda /l/) — `/bɔl/`
  2. **Car** (Coda /r/) — `/kɑɹ/`
  3. **Bell** (Coda /l/) — `/bɛl/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear complete consonant closure at the end of the word (*not dropping into "baw" or "kah"*).

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of coda tongue lift (/l/) and curl (/r/).
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Beach Clean-Up** — Selecting words ending with full liquid codas.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Building `B + ALL -> BALL`.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I kick the baw"* -> Child fixes: *"ball!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I play with the ball."*, *"The car is fast."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `al`, `el`, `ar`, `or`
- **Words**: `ball`, `car`, `bell`, `door`, `bear`
- **Phrases**: `a red ball`, `a big car`, `a loud bell`
- **Sentences**: `I kick the red ball.`, `Drive the car.`, `Ring the loud bell.`\n
