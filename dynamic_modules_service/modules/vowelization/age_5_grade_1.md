# Learning Module: Vowelization of Post-Vocalic Liquids (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `vowelization-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Vowelization (/l, r/ coda -> [o, u, ə])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~5;0–6;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-3`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.2 Family Rhyme Porch)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-3` (Isolate ending consonant sounds).
- **How It Works**: Three Grade 1 images appear:
  1. **Ball** (Coda /l/) — `/bɔl/`
  2. **Car** (Coda /r/) — `/kɑɹ/`
  3. **Door** (Coda /r/) — `/dɔɹ/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to contrast coda liquid articulation vs vowel endings.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Hearing distinct coda closure vs open vowel.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Rhyme Porch** — Rhyme sorting with coda liquids.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Guided Training Path** — Multi-word guided repetitions.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Forest Discovery** — Independent identification.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"My father closes the door."*, *"We wash the car."*

---

- **Words**: `ball`, `car`, `door`, `bell`, `bear`, `water`
- **Phrases**: `open the door`, `a red ball`, `wash the car`
