# Learning Module: Gliding of /l/ (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `gliding-l-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Gliding of /l/ (/l/ -> [w, j])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~5;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 3: *"Family & Healthy Food"* (Level 3.1 Family Tree Sorting)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 7, 8 (Letter L, lateral tongue-tip elevation).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Lion** (Letter **L**) — `/laɪ.ən/`
  2. **Leaf** (Letter **L**) — `/lif/`
  3. **Lamp** (Letter **L**) — `/læmp/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear lateral tongue-bracing: *"L says /l/... lll-lion! Lion!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of tongue tip touching the top teeth ridge without lip rounding.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.1 Sound Bucket** — Sorting tongue-lifting /l/ (*lion*, *leaf*) from lip-rounding /w/ (*water*, *window*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.2 Build & Say** — Guided imitation: *"Put your tongue up behind your teeth and sing: lll-leaf"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.3 Forest Discovery** — Naming leaves on the path.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.4 Talk With Buddy** — *"The lion is big."*, *"I see a green leaf."*

---
