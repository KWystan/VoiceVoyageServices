# Learning Module: 2-Element Cluster Reduction (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `cluster-reduction-2el-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Cluster Reduction (2-Element Blends -> Singleton)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~4;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 3: *"Family & Healthy Food"* (Level 3.3 Cluster Garden)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 8, 23 (2-letter blend synthesis: S-blends, L-blends).
- **How It Works**: Three blend images appear:
  1. **Star** (Blend **ST**) — `/stɑɹ/`
  2. **Blue** (Blend **BL**) — `/blu/`
  3. **Frog** (Blend **FR**) — `/fɹɔɡ/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear both consonants in the blend: *"S-T says /st/... st-star! Star!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of both consonants sounding together.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.3 Sound Bucket** — Sorting blend words (*star*, *blue*) from singletons (*tar*, *boo*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.3 Build & Say** — Assembling `S + TAR -> STAR`. Modeled prompt: *"Say sss-tar"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"Look at the tar in the sky!"* -> Child fixes: *"star!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"The star is yellow."*, *"The frog can jump."*

---
