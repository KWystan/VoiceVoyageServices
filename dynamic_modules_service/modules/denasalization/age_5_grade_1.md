# Learning Module: Denasalization (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `denasalization-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Denasalization (/m, n, ŋ/ -> [b, d, ɡ])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental — Suppression ~2;6)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-3`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 3: *"Problem-Solver Market"* (Level 3.1 Market Grocery Sort)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-1` (Nasal consonants in Grade 1 words).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Moon** (Letter **M**) — `/mun/`
  2. **Nut** (Letter **N**) — `/nʌt/`
  3. **Muffin** (Letter **M**) — `/mʌf.ɪn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to practice velopharyngeal nasal resonance.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Nasal vs oral acoustic contrast.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.1 Market Grocery Sort** — Sorting nasal grocery items (*muffin*, *nut*, *mango*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.2 Price Tag Puzzle** — Modeled guided humming imitation.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.3 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I want a dut"* -> Child fixes: *"nut!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.4 Friendly Shopkeeper** — *"Can I buy a fresh muffin?"*, *"The moon is bright tonight."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `ma`, `moo`, `na`, `noo`
- **Words**: `moon`, `nut`, `muffin`, `man`, `nose`, `sun`, `banana`
- **Phrases**: `a fresh muffin`, `a brown nut`, `the bright moon`
- **Sentences**: `We buy a fresh muffin at the market.`, `The squirrel eats a brown nut.`, `The bright moon is in the sky.`\n
