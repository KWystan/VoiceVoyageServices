# Learning Module: Denasalization (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `denasalization-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Denasalization (/m, n, ŋ/ -> [b, d, ɡ])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental — Suppression ~2;6)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 2: *"My Body & My Feelings"* (Level 2.3 Senses & Hygiene Trail)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 8, 23 (Nasal resonance M and N; humming airflow through nose).
- **How It Works**: Three nasal images appear:
  1. **Moon** (Nasal /m/) — `/mun/`
  2. **Nut** (Nasal /n/) — `/nʌt/`
  3. **Muffin** (Nasal /m/) — `/mʌf.ɪn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to feel nasal humming vibration: *"M says /m/... mmm-moon! NOT boon!"*, *"N says /n/... nnn-nut! NOT dut!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Visual animation showing airflow humming through the nose.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **2.3 Sound Bucket** — Sorting humming nasal words (*moon*, *nut*) from popping oral stops (*boon*, *duck*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **2.3 Build & Say** — Building `M + OON -> MOON`. Model prompt: *"Hum like a bee with your lips closed: mmm-moon"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **2.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I see the boon in the sky"* -> Child fixes: *"moon!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **2.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"The yellow moon shines."*, *"I eat a sweet muffin."*

---
