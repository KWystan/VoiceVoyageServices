# Learning Module: Weak Syllable Deletion (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `wsd-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Weak Syllable Deletion (WSD) / Multi-Syllable Precision  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: Clinically Significant Delay (Past ~4;0 90% Suppression Milestone)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy Standards (`RL1PA-I-2`, `RL1PA-I-5`, `RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"*  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework

### 1.1 MATATAG Curriculum Focus
- **`RL1PA-I-2`**: Segment a two- to three-syllable word into its syllabic parts.
- **`RL1PA-I-5`**: Identify initial sounds (vowels, consonants, semi-vowels) in words.
- **`RL1PWS-I-1`**: Produce the sound of the letters of L1/English.
- **`RL1PWS-I-5`**: Sound out words accurately in grade-level vocabulary.
- **Thematic Context**: Family identity, home technology, nature discovery, and community places.

### 1.2 How It Works (Level 1.1 Gameplay Mechanics)
- **Visual Display**: Three familiar Grade 1 thematic images and letters appear:
  1. **Computer** (Letter **C**) — `/kəm.pju.tɚ/` (3 syllables)
  2. **Umbrella** (Letter **U**) — `/ʌm.bɹɛl.ə/` (3 syllables)
  3. **Cucumber** (Letter **C**) — `/kju.kʌm.bɚ/` (3 syllables)
- **Interaction**: The child taps all three images/letters.
- **Audio Feedback**: Tapping highlights the letter, sounds out the initial phoneme, and clearly segments each syllable beat with glowing visual pulses:
  - *"C says /k/! com... PU... ter! Computer!"*
  - *"U says /ʌ/! um... BREL... la! Umbrella!"*
  - *"C says /k/! cu... CUM... ber! Cucumber!"*
- **Speech Goal**: Build phonological awareness of unstressed initial syllables (e.g. *com-* `/kəm/`, *um-* `/ʌm/`) so the child produces the complete 3-syllable acoustic contour without reducing to *"pyuter"* or *"brella"*.

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Speech-Learning Progression

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        5-Stage Gameplay Architecture for WSD (Grade 1)                 │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬───────────────────┬────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 1: Listen   │ Stage 2: Recognize│ Stage 3: Guided   │ Stage 4: Independent       │
│ Sound & Name Stn  │ Syllable Porch    │ Community Walk    │ Jungle Hunt & Monster      │
│ (3 Images Tap)    │ (Syllable Claps)  │ (Pacing Path)     │ (Fixing Dropped Syllables) │
├───────────────────┴───────────────────┴───────────────────┴────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 5: Use in Context — Family Story Time & Roleplay (Complete Sentence Fluency)     │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Stage 1: Listen (Receptive Acoustic & Prosodic Exposure)
- **Mechanic**: **1.1 Sound Station / Picture Listen**
- **Action**: 3 target pictures (*Computer*, *Umbrella*, *Cucumber*) appear. Tapping produces stressed-unstressed prosodic waveforms showing all 3 vocalic peaks.
- **Speech Demand**: Receptive only.

### Stage 2: Recognize (Receptive Syllable Segmentation)
- **Mechanic**: **1.2 Family Syllable Porch / Sound Bucket**
- **Action**: App asks: *"Find the word with 3 syllable claps!"*. Child selects *Computer* over single-syllable *Cat* or 2-syllable *Pencil*.
- **Speech Demand**: Receptive discrimination aligned with MATATAG `RL1PA-I-2`.

### Stage 3: Guided Say (Modeled Multi-Syllable Imitation)
- **Mechanic**: **1.3 Community Walk / Build & Say**
- **Action**: Child snaps 3 syllable blocks together (`com` + `pu` + `ter` $ightarrow$ `computer`). Audio model plays with a metronome beat: *"com-pu-ter"*. Child imitates and records voice.
- **Speech Demand**: Modeled speech production.

### Stage 4: Independent Say (Spontaneous Word Retrieval & Error Correction)
- **Mechanic**: **1.4 Forest Discovery & Silly Monster**
- **Action**: Silly Monster says *"Look at my pyuter!"* or *"Take the brella!"*. Prompt: *"Monster forgot a syllable! Say the whole word to teach him!"*. Child independently speaks: *"computer"* and *"umbrella"*.
- **Speech Demand**: Spontaneous independent naming.

### Stage 5: Use in Context (Connected Sentence Fluency & Discourse)
- **Mechanic**: **Family Story Time & Mission Roleplay**
- **Action**: Branching dialogue and sentence production:
  - *"My sister uses the computer."*
  - *"I open my green umbrella."*
  - *"My family eats fresh cucumber."*
- **Speech Demand**: Connected discourse in full multi-syllabic sentences.

## 4. Clinical Rationale for Module Generation
Grade 1 practice plan for Weak Syllable Deletion (WSD). At Age 5 (Grade 1), WSD is clinically delayed as empirical 90% acquisition norms (Crowe & McLeod, 2020) indicate suppression by ~4;0. Mapped to DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Reading Literacy standards (`RL1PA-I-2`, `RL1PWS-I-5`). Implements full 5-stage scaffolding starting from Level 1.1 Sound Station (3-image tapping: Computer, Umbrella, Cucumber) through syllable block assembly, Silly Monster repair, and connected sentence reading fluency.
