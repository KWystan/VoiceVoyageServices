# Learning Module: Weak Syllable Deletion (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `wsd-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Weak Syllable Deletion (WSD)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Resolving ~4;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 15, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"*  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework

### 1.1 MATATAG Curriculum Focus
- **Competency 7**: Identify one's given name, friends' names, and familiar school/household objects.
- **Competency 8**: Produce the sound of the letter it stands for.
- **Competency 15**: Segment a two- to three-syllable word into syllables.
- **Competency 23**: Recognize that sounding off letters forms words.
- **Thematic Context**: Self-identity, name spelling, personal belongings, and familiar classroom objects.

### 1.2 How It Works (Level 1.1 Gameplay Mechanics)
- **Visual Display**: Three familiar images with their starting letters appear on the screen:
  1. **Banana** (Letter **B**) — `/bə.næ.nə/` (3 syllables)
  2. **Elephant** (Letter **E**) — `/ɛl.ə.fənt/` (3 syllables)
  3. **Tomato** (Letter **T**) — `/tə.meɪ.toʊ/` (3 syllables)
- **Interaction**: The child taps each of the three images/letters.
- **Audio Feedback**: Tapping plays the clear pronunciation of the multisyllabic word with acoustic stretching and visual highlighting of the weak initial/medial unstressed syllable:
  - *"b-b-banana! bə... NA... nə!"*
  - *"e-e-elephant! EL... ə... fənt!"*
  - *"t-t-tomato! tə... MEI... toʊ!"*
- **Speech Goal**: Familiarize the child with the full 3-beat metrical rhythm of words so that unstressed syllables (e.g. *bə-*, *tə-*, *-ə-*) are not dropped.

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Speech-Learning Progression

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        5-Stage Gameplay Architecture for WSD                           │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬───────────────────┬────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 1: Listen   │ Stage 2: Recognize│ Stage 3: Guided   │ Stage 4: Independent       │
│ Name & Letter Stn │ Classroom Bucket  │ Syllable Bridge   │ Forest Discovery           │
│ (3 Images Tap)    │ (Syllable Sorting)│ (Echo with Beats) │ (Correcting Silly Monster) │
├───────────────────┴───────────────────┴───────────────────┴────────────────────────────┤
│ Stage 5: Use in Context — Story Adventure & Talk With Buddy (Sentence Fluency)         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Stage 1: Listen (Auditory Familiarization & Rhythm Exposure)
- **Mechanic**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station / Picture Listen**
- **Action**: 3 familiar items (*Banana*, *Elephant*, *Tomato*) appear. Child taps all three to hear word models with synchronized syllable-pulse glows.
- **Speech Demand**: Receptive only (No speaking required).

### Stage 2: Recognize (Syllable Discrimination & Counting)
- **Mechanic**: **1.2 Classroom Bag Collector / Sound Bucket**
- **Action**: Child listens to spoken audio bubbles (*"ba-na-na"*, *"pen-cil"*, *"el-e-phant"*) and drags them into the **2-Clap Bucket** (*Pencil*, *Paper*, *Baby*) or **3-Clap Bucket** (*Banana*, *Elephant*, *Tomato*).
- **Speech Demand**: Receptive discrimination / auditory syllable counting.

### Stage 3: Guided Say (Modeled Imitation with Syllable Beats)
- **Mechanic**: **1.3 Syllable Bridge Crossing / Build & Say**
- **Action**: Syllable stepping stones light up one by one (`ba` $\rightarrow$ `na` $\rightarrow$ `na`). Voice Voyage model speaks: *"Say ba-na-na!"*. Child repeats into the microphone while tapping the rhythm dots.
- **Speech Demand**: Modeled speech production.

### Stage 4: Independent Say (Spontaneous Word Retrieval)
- **Mechanic**: **1.4 Forest Discovery & Silly Monster**
- **Action**: The Silly Monster intentionally makes a weak syllable deletion error (says *"nana"* for *banana* or *"el-phant"* for *elephant*). The child is prompted: *"Uh oh! Monster dropped a syllable! Say banana to help him!"*.
- **Speech Demand**: Spontaneous independent naming.

### Stage 5: Use in Context (Connected Speech & Carrier Phrases)
- **Mechanic**: **Talk With Buddy & Story Adventure**
- **Action**: Interactive dialogue with Voice Voyage mascot:
  - Mascot: *"What is in your school bag?"*
  - Child: *"I have a banana."*
  - Mascot: *"What animal do you see?"*
  - Child: *"The big elephant."*
- **Speech Demand**: Connected discourse in complete carrier phrases and sentences.

## 4. Clinical Rationale for Module Generation
Kindergarten practice plan for Weak Syllable Deletion (WSD). Grounded in Crowe & McLeod (2020) suppression norms (~4;0) and DepEd MATATAG Key Stage 1 Kindergarten Literacy Standards (Competencies 7, 8, 15, 23). Uses 5-stage scaffolding starting from Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station (3-image tapping) to structured syllable counting, imitation with visual beats, error correction against Silly Monster, and connected carrier phrases.
