# Learning Module: Initial Consonant Deletion (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `icd-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Initial Consonant Deletion (ICD: CV -> V)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competency 23: Beginning Sound Awareness)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competency 23 (Isolating initial onset sounds; establishing consonant anchor before vowel).
- **How It Works**: Three high-visual onset images appear:
  1. **Pig** (Onset /p/) — `/pɪɡ/`
  2. **Ball** (Onset /b/) — `/bɔl/`
  3. **Sun** (Onset /s/) — `/sʌn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear the prominent starting consonant burst before the vowel (*"p-p-pig! NOT ig!"*, *"b-b-ball! NOT all!"*, *"s-s-sun! NOT un!"*).

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio-visual bouncing animations highlighting the first letter contact.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Bucket** — Sorting words with starting consonants (*pig*, *ball*) vs vowel-only words (*egg*, *apple*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Snapping initial consonant tile `P + IG -> PIG` with prompt *"Close your lips for P before saying ig: p-pig"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I see an ig!"* -> Child fixes: *"pig!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"The pink pig plays."*, *"I kick the ball."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `pa`, `ba`, `sa`, `ta`
- **Words**: `pig`, `ball`, `sun`, `cup`, `ten`, `dog`
- **Phrases**: `a pink pig`, `a big ball`, `a bright sun`
- **Sentences**: `The pig is pink.`, `I have a big ball.`, `The sun is hot.`\n
