# Learning Module: Initial Consonant Deletion (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `icd-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Initial Consonant Deletion (ICD: CV -> V)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PA-I-5`, `RL1PWS-I-3`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.1 Sound Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PA-I-5` (Identify initial sounds in Grade 1 words).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Leaf** (Onset /l/) — `/lif/`
  2. **Ring** (Onset /r/) — `/ɹɪŋ/`
  3. **Key** (Onset /k/) — `/ki/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to emphasize consonant onset anchoring.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Continuous onset highlighting.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Rhyme Porch** — Finding words with target initial consonants.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Community Walk** — Guided consonant-vowel blending (`L + EAF`).
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"Look at the eaf"* -> Child fixes: *"leaf!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"The green leaf falls."*, *"I wear a gold ring."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `la`, `ree`, `ka`
- **Words**: `leaf`, `ring`, `key`, `cat`, `dog`, `sun`
- **Phrases**: `a green leaf`, `a gold ring`, `a small key`
- **Sentences**: `I see a green leaf.`, `My mother has a ring.`, `The key opens the door.`\n
