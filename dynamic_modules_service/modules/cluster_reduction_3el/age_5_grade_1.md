**Module ID**: `cluster-reduction-3el-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Cluster Reduction (3-Element Blends -> Singleton/Doubleton)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~5;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-4`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 2: *"Nature & Animals Trail"* (Level 2.2 Jungle Blend Crossing)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-1` (3-consonant clusters), `RL1PWS-I-4` (Blend substitution).
- **How It Works**: Three Grade 1 images appear:
  1. **Street** (Letters **STR**) — `/stɹit/`
  2. **Spring** (Letters **SPR**) — `/spɹɪŋ/`
  3. **Straw** (Letters **STR**) — `/stɹɔ/`
- **Interaction**: Taps all 3 to isolate /s/ + stop + liquid sequencing.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Receptive 3-element blend discrimination.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **2.2 Jungle Blend Crossing** — Sorting 3-element blends (*street*, *spring*) from 2-element blends (*star*, *spider*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **2.2 Build the Bridge** — Step-by-step modeled blend imitation.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **2.3 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I walk on the teet"* -> Child corrects: *"street!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **2.4 Safari Explorer Log** — *"We walk down our street."*, *"I drink water with a straw."*
