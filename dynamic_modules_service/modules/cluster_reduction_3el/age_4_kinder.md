# Learning Module: 3-Element Cluster Reduction (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `cluster-reduction-3el-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Cluster Reduction (3-Element Blends -> Singleton/Doubleton)  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~5;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 3: *"Family & Healthy Food"* (Level 3.4 Sound Path Jumper)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 8, 23 (Complex 3-consonant blends /str, spr, skr/).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Street** (Letters **STR**) — `/stɹit/`
  2. **Spring** (Letters **SPR**) — `/spɹɪŋ/`
  3. **Straw** (Letters **STR**) — `/stɹɔ/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear the 3-sound sequence: *"S-T-R says /str/... str-street! Street!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of triple-consonant onset.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.4 Sound Bucket** — Identifying complex cluster words.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.4 Build & Say** — Building `STR + EET -> STREET`.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.4 Build the Bridge** — Independently naming displayed 3-blend words to build planks.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"The car is on the street."*, *"I drink with a straw."*
