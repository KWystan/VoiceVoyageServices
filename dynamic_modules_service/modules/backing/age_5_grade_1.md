# Learning Module: Backing (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `backing-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Backing (/t, d, s/ -> [k, ɡ, ʃ])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-3`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.1 Sound Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-3` (Alveolar sound isolation).
- **How It Works**: Three Grade 1 images appear:
  1. **Ten** (Letter **T**) — `/tɛn/`
  2. **Door** (Letter **D**) — `/dɔɹ/`
  3. **Sun** (Letter **S**) — `/sʌn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to practice front alveolar tongue placement.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Receptive front vs back discrimination.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Rhyme Porch** — Identifying words starting with front alveolar sounds.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Community Walk** — Guided tongue-tip placement repetition.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"Look at the kun"* -> Child fixes: *"sun!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"We open the wooden door."*, *"The bright sun shines."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `ta`, `to`, `da`, `door`, `sa`
- **Words**: `ten`, `door`, `sun`, `duck`, `dog`, `soap`
- **Phrases**: `open the door`, `a warm sun`, `ten green ducks`
- **Sentences**: `I count ten green ducks.`, `My father opens the door.`, `The sun is very warm.`\n
