# Learning Module: Backing (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `backing-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Backing (/t, d, s/ -> [k, ɡ, ʃ])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 2: *"My Body & My Feelings"* (Level 2.2 Body Parts Puzzle)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 8, 23 (Front teeth-ridge placement /t, d/ vs throat sounds /k, ɡ/).
- **How It Works**: Three front alveolar images appear:
  1. **Ten** (Alveolar /t/) — `/tɛn/`
  2. **Dog** (Alveolar /d/) — `/dɔɡ/`
  3. **Toe** (Alveolar /t/) — `/toʊ/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear front tongue-tip tap: *"T says /t/... t-t-ten! NOT ken!"*, *"D says /d/... d-d-dog! NOT gog!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Visual animation showing tongue-tip tapping behind upper teeth.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **2.2 Sound Bucket** — Sorting front sounds (*ten*, *dog*, *toe*) from back sounds (*ken*, *gog*, *car*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **2.2 Build & Say** — Building `T + EN -> TEN`. Modeled cue: *"Touch your tongue tip right behind your front teeth: t-ten"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **2.3 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I have a gog"* -> Child fixes: *"dog!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **2.4 Echo Cave** — *"I have ten toes."*, *"My dog is friendly."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `ta`, `tee`, `to`, `da`, `dee`, `do`
- **Words**: `ten`, `dog`, `toe`, `tea`, `duck`, `door`
- **Phrases**: `ten little toes`, `a friendly dog`, `a yellow duck`
- **Sentences**: `I count to ten.`, `My dog runs to me.`, `I open the front door.`\n
