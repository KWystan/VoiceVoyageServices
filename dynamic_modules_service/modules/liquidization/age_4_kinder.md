# Learning Module: Liquidization (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `liquidization-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Liquidization (/w, j/ -> [l, r])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 8, 23 (Simple glide lip rounding /w/ and palatal glide /j/).
- **How It Works**: Three glide images appear:
  1. **Water** (Glide /w/) — `/wɔ.tɚ/`
  2. **Yellow** (Glide /j/) — `/jɛl.oʊ/`
  3. **Window** (Glide /w/) — `/wɪn.doʊ/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to practice smooth lip rounding without tongue elevation: *"W says /w/... w-w-water! NOT roter!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of smooth glide airflow.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Bucket** — Distinguishing glides (*water*, *yellow*) from liquids (*red*, *lion*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Building `W + ATER -> WATER`. Model prompt: *"Round your lips like kissing and say w-water"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I want some roter"* -> Child fixes: *"water!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I drink clean water."*, *"The yellow sun shines."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `wa`, `wee`, `ya`, `yo`
- **Words**: `water`, `yellow`, `window`, `web`, `yes`
- **Phrases**: `clean water`, `a yellow duck`, `open the window`
