# Learning Module: Liquidization (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `liquidization-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Liquidization (/w, j/ -> [l, r])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: **Red Flag (Atypical / Non-Developmental)**  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.1 Sound Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-1` (Semi-vowels /w, j/ sounds).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Water** (Letter **W**) — `/wɔ.tɚ/`
  2. **Yellow** (Letter **Y**) — `/jɛl.oʊ/`
  3. **Window** (Letter **W**) — `/wɪn.doʊ/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to isolate glide sounds.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Audio modeling of lip-rounding glides.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Rhyme Porch** — Glide identification in connected speech.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Community Walk** — Modeled guided path repetition.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I see a lellow bird"* -> Child fixes: *"yellow!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"We look out the clean window."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `wa`, `wee`, `ya`, `yo`
- **Words**: `water`, `yellow`, `window`, `web`, `yes`
- **Phrases**: `clean cool water`, `a bright yellow sun`, `the classroom window`
- **Sentences**: `We drink clean cool water.`, `The bright yellow sun rises.`, `I open the classroom window.`\n
