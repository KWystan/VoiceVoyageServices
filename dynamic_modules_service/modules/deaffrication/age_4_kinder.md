# Learning Module: Deaffrication (/tʃ, dʒ/) (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `deaffrication-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Deaffrication (/tʃ, dʒ/ -> [ʃ, s, t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~4;6)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 7, 8 (Affricate digraphs CH and J).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Chair** (Letters **CH**) — `/tʃɛɹ/`
  2. **Jump** (Letter **J**) — `/dʒʌmp/`
  3. **Chicken** (Letters **CH**) — `/tʃɪk.ən/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear the explosive two-part affricate release (*stop + friction burst*): *"CH says /tʃ/... ch-chair! Chair!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Highlighting the rapid "t + sh" burst closure.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Bucket** — Sorting affricates (*chair*, *jump*) from plain fricatives (*share*, *sun*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Assembling `CH + AIR -> CHAIR`. Audio model: *"Start with t and burst into sh: chhh-air"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Forest Discovery** — Finding hidden classroom chairs.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I sit on the chair."*, *"I like to jump."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `cha`, `chee`, `cho`, `chu`, `ja`, `jee`, `jo`
- **Words**: `chair`, `chicken`, `cheese`, `jump`, `juice`, `jar`
- **Phrases**: `a big chair`, `a little chicken`, `a cup of juice`
