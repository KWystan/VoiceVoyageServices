# Learning Module: Devoicing of Codas (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `devoicing-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Devoicing (/b, d, ɡ, z/ -> [p, t, k, s])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Resolving ~3;0–4;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.3 Sound Beach Clean-Up)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 7, 8 (Coda voicing awareness on voiced stops /b, d, ɡ/).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Bed** (Coda **D**) — `/bɛd/`
  2. **Pig** (Coda **G**) — `/pɪɡ/`
  3. **Dog** (Coda **G**) — `/dɔɡ/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to feel the buzzing throat vibration at the end of the word (*bed, not bet; pig, not pick*).

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of voiced coda releases.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.3 Sound Beach Clean-Up** — Distinguishing *bed* (/bɛd/) vs *bet* (/bɛt/) and *pig* (/pɪɡ/) vs *pick* (/pɪk/).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Building `B + ED -> BED`. Child touches throat to feel voice buzz.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I sleep in a bet"* -> Child fixes: *"bed!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I have a soft bed."*, *"The little pig is pink."*

---
