# Learning Module: Stopping of /v/ (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `stopping-v-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/v/ -> [b])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~5;6)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competency 8 (Letter sound /v/), Competency 23 (Sounding off words).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Van** (Letter **V**) — `/væn/`
  2. **Vest** (Letter **V**) — `/vɛst/`
  3. **Vase** (Letter **V**) — `/veɪs/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all three to hear buzzing friction on upper teeth and lower lip: *"V says /v/... vvv-van! Van!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Tapping *Van*, *Vest*, *Vase* to hear continuous lip-teeth buzzing.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Bucket** — Distinguishing popping /b/ (*ball*, *bed*) vs buzzing /v/ (*van*, *vest*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Building `V + AN -> VAN` with model voice *"Touch your teeth to your lip and say vvan"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Forest Discovery** — Revealing hidden van under trees and naming independently.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I see a blue van."*

---
