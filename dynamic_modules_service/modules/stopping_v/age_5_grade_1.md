# Learning Module: Stopping of /v/ (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `stopping-v-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/v/ -> [b])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~5;6)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-3`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 3: *"Problem-Solver Market"* (Level 3.1 Market Grocery Sort)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-1` (Letter sounds), `RL1PWS-I-3` (Isolate beginning sounds), Market items.
- **How It Works**: Three Grade 1 market items appear:
  1. **Van** (Letter **V**) — `/væn/`
  2. **Vegetable** (Letter **V**) — `/vɛdʒ.tə.bəl/`
  3. **Vine** (Letter **V**) — `/vaɪn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear /v/ labiodental friction isolation and blending.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Receptive exposure to /v/ vs /b/ acoustic contrasts.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.1 Market Grocery Sort** — Sorting /v/ market goods into delivery vans.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.2 Price Tag Puzzle** — Assembling words with guided audio model prompts.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.3 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I want a ban!"* -> Child corrects: *"van!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.4 Friendly Shopkeeper** — *"Can I ride in the delivery van?"*, *"We buy fresh vegetables."*

---
