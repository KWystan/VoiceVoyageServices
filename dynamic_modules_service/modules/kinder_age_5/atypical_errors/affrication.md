## 1. Island Level Gameplay Guidelines

### Island 1: "My School & My World" (Weeks 1-2)
**MATATAG Focus**: Self-Identity, Name, Colors, Shapes, Spatial Positions

| Level | MATATAG Focus | New Gameplay | How it Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1.1 Name & Letter Station** | Name spelling, initial letter recognition, self-identity. | Step 1 - Picture Listen | Three familiar images/letters appear. The child taps all three to hear the word and emphasized initial sound. |
| **1.2 Classroom Bag Collector** | Classroom routines, sorting items by colors and shapes. | Step 2 - Sound Bucket | A sound bubble plays a classroom item. The child drags it into the matching image bucket. Complete 3 correct bubbles. |
| **1.3 Sound Beach Clean-Up** | Spatial concepts and object classification. | Step 2 - Find the Sound | The app asks for a word that ends with a target sound. Four beach-item pictures appear. Complete 2 correct rounds. |
| **1.4 Syllable Bridge Crossing** | Number sense 1-3, step counting, spatial navigation. | Step 3 - Guided Training Path | The child hears a target word, repeats it, and each valid attempt lets the character cross one stepping-stone obstacle. |

| **5.4 Grand Story Ceremony** | Evaluates overall speech intelligibility and connected narrative speech fluency. | Step 5 - Story Adventure | The child completes a short story and records final summary sentences as the last scene. |
