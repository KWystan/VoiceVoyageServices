## 1. Island Level Gameplay Guidelines

| **1.2 Classroom Bag Collector** | Classroom routines, sorting items by colors and shapes. | Step 2 - Sound Bucket | A sound bubble plays a classroom item. The child drags it into the matching image bucket. Complete 3 correct bubbles. |

## 3. 5-Stage Gameplay Speech-Learning Progression

- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** â€” Auditory contrast of complete stop closures vs leaky friction sounds (*pen* vs *fen*).
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Classroom Bag Collector** â€” Identifies solid stop air blockage.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.4 Syllable Bridge Crossing** â€” Blends `T + OP -> TOP` with firm alveolar stop burst.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.4 Sound Path Jumper** â€” Independently names *"pen"*, *"pig"*, *"ten"*, *"top"*, *"cup"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **4.4 Sound Explorer Gate** â€” Answers: *"Top spins fast."* (3 words)
