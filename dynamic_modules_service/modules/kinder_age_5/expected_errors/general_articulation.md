## 1. Island Level Gameplay Guidelines

### Island 1: "My School & My World" (Weeks 1-2)
**MATATAG Focus**: Self-Identity, Name, Colors, Shapes, Spatial Positions

| Level | MATATAG Focus | New Gameplay | How it Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1.1 Name & Letter Station** | Name spelling, initial letter recognition, self-identity. | Step 1 - Picture Listen | Three familiar images/letters appear. The child taps all three to hear the word and emphasized initial sound. |
| **1.2 Classroom Bag Collector** | Classroom routines, sorting items by colors and shapes. | Step 2 - Sound Bucket | A sound bubble plays a classroom item. The child drags it into the matching image bucket. Complete 3 correct bubbles. |
| **1.3 Sound Beach Clean-Up** | Spatial concepts and object classification. | Step 2 - Find the Sound | The app asks for a word that ends with a target sound. Four beach-item pictures appear. Complete 2 correct rounds. |
| **1.4 Syllable Bridge Crossing** | Number sense 1-3, step counting, spatial navigation. | Step 3 - Guided Training Path | The child hears a target word, repeats it, and each valid attempt lets the character cross one stepping-stone obstacle. |

### Island 2: "My Body & My Feelings" (Weeks 3-6)
**MATATAG Focus**: Emotions, Body Parts, 5 Senses, Hygiene, Numbers 1-5

| Level | MATATAG Focus | New Gameplay | How it Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2.1 Emotion Mirror Lagoon** | Identifying basic feelings (Happy, Sad, Angry, Scared, Surprised). | Step 2 - Find the Word | The app asks for an emotion/target word and shows 4 images. Child selects the correct one; complete 2 rounds. |
| **2.2 Body Parts Puzzle** | Identifying body parts (Head, Hand, Foot, Leg, Knee, Ear, Eye, Nose). | Step 3 - Build & Say | Child completes the target word, hears the correct model, then repeats it into the mic. |
| **2.3 Senses & Hygiene Trail** | The 5 senses, hygiene habits, quantities 1-5. | Step 3 - Listen, Pop & Repeat | Child pops hygiene bubbles. Each word plays, then 'Your turn' prompts a spoken attempt. |
| **2.4 Echo Cave (Voice Studio)** | Verbal self-expression and simple phrases. | Step 5 - Talk With Buddy | Buddy asks short structured questions. Child responds using simple target-rich phrases. |

### Island 3: "Family & Healthy Food" (Weeks 7-8)
**MATATAG Focus**: Family Roles, Small vs. Big Family, Healthy Foods (Go, Grow, Glow)

| Level | MATATAG Focus | New Gameplay | How it Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3.1 Family Tree Sorting** | Family roles and family size. | Step 2 - Sound Bucket | A spoken family/target word appears as a sound bubble. Child drags it to the matching picture bucket. Complete 3 correct matches. |
| **3.2 Healthy Plate Market** | Nutrition and food groups (Go, Grow, Glow). | Step 4 - Forest Discovery (Market Theme) | Child reveals hidden market items from crates/baskets. App asks 'What did you find?' Child names the item without hearing it first. |
| **3.3 Cluster Garden** | Environmental objects and counting food sets 1-5. | Step 3 - Build & Say | Child assembles the blend/word first, then hears the model and says it. |
| **3.4 Sound Path Jumper** | Ordinal concepts and counting 1-5. | Step 4 - Build the Bridge | Each independently spoken target word pops a word bubble and creates one bridge segment. Character crosses when all pieces are built. |

### Island 4: "Home & Story Land" (Weeks 9-10)
**MATATAG Focus**: Calendar Dates, Days of Week, Ordinals 1st-3rd, Story Sequencing, Numbers 0-10

| Level | MATATAG Focus | New Gameplay | How it Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **4.1 Calendar Castle** | Days of the week, birthday dates, number counting 0-10. | Step 2 - Find the Sound | The app asks for a number/calendar word containing the target sound. Four pictures or number cards appear. Child selects the correct one. |
| **4.2 Story Sequencer (Three Little Pigs)** | Story comprehension and narrative sequencing. | Step 5 - Story Adventure | Child follows the story and answers target-rich prompts to unlock the next scene. |
| **4.3 Rhyme House Match** | Auditory rhyming awareness and target-letter vocabulary. | Step 2 - Find the Word | The app plays/asks for one word and shows 4 picture choices, including the intended contrast. Child completes 2 correct rounds. |
| **4.4 Sound Explorer Gate** | Full Quarter 1 synthesis and cumulative review. | Step 5 - Mission Roleplay | The child completes short communication missions to unlock gate sections using customized target-rich phrases. |

#### Post-Assessment Capstone
### Island 5: "Mastery Island" (Post-Assessment)
**Purpose**: Fully interactive post-assessment embedded directly into gameplay mechanics to evaluate overall progress without formal test stress.

| Level | Practice Objective | New Gameplay | How it Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **5.1 Echo Waterfall** | Re-evaluates early acquisition sounds (/p, b, m, n, t, d/), ICD, and FCD. | Step 4 - Forest Discovery | Hidden target items are revealed one at a time. The child names each item independently without a model immediately beforehand. |
| **5.2 Magic Mirror** | Re-evaluates velar vs. alveolar substitution (/k, É¡/ vs. /t, d/). | Step 4 - Silly Monster | The monster intentionally says a target incorrectly. The child gives the correct word; successful corrections fill the friendship meter. |
| **5.3 Fricative & Cluster Bridge** | Re-evaluates Age 5 fricatives (/s, z, f, Êƒ/) and S/L clusters. | Step 4 - Build the Bridge | Each independent target production creates one bridge segment. The character crosses after all required words are completed. |
| **5.4 Grand Story Ceremony** | Evaluates overall speech intelligibility and connected narrative speech fluency. | Step 5 - Story Adventure | The child completes a short story and records final summary sentences as the last scene. |
