- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** â€” Auditory contrast of rhotic /r/ vs labiovelar glide /w/ in minimal pairs (*red* vs *wed*, *run* vs *one*).
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.3 Sound Beach Clean-Up** â€” Identifies rhotic bunched tongue acoustic timbre.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.4 Syllable Bridge Crossing** â€” Blends `R + ED -> RED` with lateral tongue bracing.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.4 Sound Path Jumper** â€” Independently produces *"red"*, *"run"*, *"rabbit"*, *"ruler"*.
