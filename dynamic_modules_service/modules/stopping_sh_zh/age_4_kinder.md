# Learning Module: Stopping of /ʃ/ ("sh") (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `stopping-sh-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/ʃ/ -> [t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~6;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 3: *"Family & Healthy Food"* (Level 3.2 Healthy Plate Market)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competency 8 (Digraph sound "sh"), Competency 7 (Clothing and animal vocabulary).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Shoe** (Letters **SH**) — `/ʃu/`
  2. **Sheep** (Letters **SH**) — `/ʃip/`
  3. **Shell** (Letters **SH**) — `/ʃɛl/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear the quiet "shhh" wind friction: *"SH says /ʃ/... shhh-shoe! Shoe!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Hearing continuous palatal airflow models.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.2 Sound Bucket** — Sorting "sh" items (*shoe*, *shell*) from "t" items (*tea*, *toe*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.3 Build & Say** — Modeled practice: *"Round your lips and blow: shhhoe"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.4 Forest Discovery** — Finding hidden shoes on the trail.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I put on my blue shoe."*

---

- **Syllables**: `sha`, `shee`, `sho`, `shu`
- **Words**: `shoe`, `sheep`, `shell`, `ship`, `fish`
- **Phrases**: `a red shoe`, `a white sheep`, `a small shell`
