# Learning Module: Stopping of /ʃ, ʒ/ (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `stopping-sh-zh-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/ʃ, ʒ/ -> [t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~6;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-3`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 2: *"Nature & Animals Trail"* (Level 2.1 Animal Sound Safari)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-3` (Isolate beginning/ending sounds), Animals and nature.
- **How It Works**: Three animal images appear:
  1. **Sheep** (Letters **SH**) — `/ʃip/`
  2. **Fish** (Letters **SH** final) — `/fɪʃ/`
  3. **Ship** (Letters **SH**) — `/ʃɪp/`
- **Interaction**: Taps all 3 to hear /ʃ/ in initial and final positions.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Continuous friction exposure.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **2.1 Animal Safari** — Identifying animals with "sh" sounds.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **2.2 Jungle Trail** — Modeled imitation with lip-rounding cues.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **2.3 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I caught a fit!"* -> Child says *"fish!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **2.4 Safari Explorer Log** — *"The sheep eats green grass."*, *"The big ship sails."*

---

- **Words**: `sheep`, `shoe`, `fish`, `shell`, `ship`
- **Phrases**: `a soft sheep`, `a swimming fish`, `a big ship`
