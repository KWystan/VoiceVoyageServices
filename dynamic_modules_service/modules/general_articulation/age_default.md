# Learning Module: General Articulation & Speech Clarity (Universal Reserve)
**Module ID**: `general-articulation-reserve`  
**Target Process**: General Articulation & Phonological Awareness  
**Target Age / Grade**: All Ages (4 to 8) | Universal Reserve Fallback  
**Clinical Status**: General Developmental Practice  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Literacy Standards (Key Stage 1)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Key Stage 1 Island 1: *"Foundations of Speech"*  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Foundational letter-sound production and word clarity.
- **How It Works**: Three familiar foundational images appear:
  1. **Sun** (Letter **S**) — `/sʌn/`
  2. **Ball** (Letter **B**) — `/bɔl/`
  3. **Cat** (Letter **C**) — `/kæt/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear clear letter sounds and word models.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of target sounds.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Bucket** — Categorizing sounds by initial letter.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Modeled syllable and CVC word repetition.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Forest Discovery** — Independent word naming.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — Connected speech in simple carrier phrases.

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `sa`, `ba`, `ka`, `ta`
- **Words**: `sun`, `ball`, `cat`, `dog`, `bed`, `pig`
- **Phrases**: `a bright sun`, `a red ball`, `a little cat`
- **Sentences**: `The sun is bright.`, `I play with my ball.`, `The little cat sleeps.`\n
