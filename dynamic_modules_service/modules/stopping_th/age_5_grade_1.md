# Learning Module: Stopping of /θ, ð/ ("th") (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `stopping-th-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/θ, ð/ -> [t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~7;0–8;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-3`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.1 Sound Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-1` (Digraph sounds), Family vocabulary (*mother*, *father*, *feather*).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Thumb** (Voiceless /θ/) — `/θʌm/`
  2. **Feather** (Voiced /ð/) — `/fɛð.ɚ/`
  3. **Three** (Voiceless /θ/) — `/θɹi/`
- **Interaction**: Taps all 3 to hear voiced and voiceless "th" interdental airflow.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Audio modeling of /θ/ vs /t/ and /ð/ vs /d/.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Rhyme Porch** — Finding words with "th" ending (*bath*, *teeth*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Community Walk** — Modeled guided imitation with mirror feedback.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"Look at my tum"* -> Child fixes: *"thumb!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"My mother holds the soft feather."*

---

## 3. 4-Tier Practice Items
- **Syllables**: `tha`, `thee`, `tho`, `ther`
- **Words**: `thumb`, `teeth`, `three`, `feather`, `bath`
- **Phrases**: `a soft feather`, `a warm bath`, `three big books`
- **Sentences**: `I take a warm bath.`, `The bird has a feather.`, `I have three red pens.`\n
