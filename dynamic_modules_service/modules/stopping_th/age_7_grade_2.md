# Learning Module: Stopping of Voiceless /θ/ (Grade 2 / Age 7)
**Module ID**: `stopping-th-age-7-grade-2`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/θ/ -> [t])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 7 (84–95 months) | Grade 2  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Final consonant acquisition window ~7;0–8;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 2 English (`EN2PWS-II-1`, `EN2PWS-II-2`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 2 Island 3: *"Culture, Festivals & Trade"* (Level 3.1 Craft Workshop)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `EN2PWS-II-1` (Digraph mastery), Cultural items and ordinal numbers (*third*, *bath*, *cloth*).
- **How It Works**: Three cultural craft items appear:
  1. **Thumb** (Initial /θ/) — `/θʌm/`
  2. **Cloth** (Final /θ/) — `/klɔθ/`
  3. **Three** (Cluster /θr/) — `/θɹi/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to analyze interdental friction vs dental stop release.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Highlighting interdental friction in initial/medial/final positions.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **3.1 Craft Workshop** — Identifying minimal pairs (*thought* vs *taught*, *bath* vs *bat*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **3.2 Build & Say** — Producing /θ/ in coda blends and phrases.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **3.3 Forest Discovery** — Independent naming of items ending with /θ/.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **3.4 Festival Story** — *"My grandmother weaves a colorful cloth."*, *"I see three festival dancers."*
