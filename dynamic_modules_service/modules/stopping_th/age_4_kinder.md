# Learning Module: Stopping of /θ, ð/ ("th") (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `stopping-th-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/θ, ð/ -> [t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~7;0–8;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 2: *"My Body & My Feelings"* (Level 2.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 7, 8 (Body parts: thumb, teeth; letter digraph TH).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Thumb** (Letters **TH**) — `/θʌm/`
  2. **Teeth** (Letters **TH**) — `/tiθ/`
  3. **Three** (Letters **TH**) — `/θɹi/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear the soft tongue-between-teeth airflow: *"TH says /θ/... th-thumb! Thumb!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Auditory modeling of tongue-tip interdental posture.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **2.1 Sound Bucket** — Sorting "th" words from "t" words (*thumb* vs *tub*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **2.2 Build & Say** — Modeled prompt: *"Stick your tongue out a little and blow: thhhumb"*.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **2.3 Forest Discovery** — Naming body parts independently.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **2.4 Talk With Buddy** — *"I have one thumb."*, *"I brush my teeth."*

---

- **Syllables**: `tha`, `thee`, `tho`
- **Words**: `thumb`, `teeth`, `three`, `bath`
- **Phrases**: `my little thumb`, `clean white teeth`, `number three`
