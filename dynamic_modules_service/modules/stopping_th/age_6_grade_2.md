# Learning Module: Stopping of /θ, ð/ (Grade 2 / Age 6)
**Module ID**: `stopping-th-age-6-grade-2`  
**Target Process**: Stopping (/θ, ð/ -> [t, d])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 6 (72–83 months) | Grade 2  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (/ð/ mastered ~6;0; /θ/ resolving ~7;0–8;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 2 English (`EN2PWS-II-1`, `EN2PA-II-1`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 2 Island 1: *"Community Helpers & Work"* (Level 1.1 Sound Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `EN2PWS-II-1` (Consonant digraphs th/sh/ch), Community items.
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Thumb** (Voiceless /θ/) — `/θʌm/`
  2. **Path** (Voiceless /θ/ coda) — `/pæθ/`
  3. **Feather** (Voiced /ð/) — `/fɛð.ɚ/`
- **Interaction**: Taps all 3 to hear distinct interdental placement.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Receptive interdental discrimination.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Community Helper Match** — Sorting /θ/ vs /ð/ words.
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Guided Training Path** — Multi-syllable paced repetition (*fea-ther*).
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Correcting monster's stopping errors.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Story Adventure** — *"We walk along the green path."*, *"The eagle has a strong feather."*
