# Learning Module: Gliding of /r/ (Kindergarten / Age 4)
**Module ID**: `gliding-r-age-4-kinder`  
**Target Process**: Gliding of /r/ (/r/ -> [w])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 4 (48–59 months) | Kindergarten  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~6;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Kindergarten Literacy (Competencies 7, 8, 23)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Kindergarten Island 1: *"My School & My World"* (Level 1.1 Name & Letter Station)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: Competencies 7, 8 (Letter R, colors, school toys).
- **How It Works**: Three images appear:
  1. **Red** (Letter **R**) — `/ɹɛd/`
  2. **Ring** (Letter **R**) — `/ɹɪŋ/`
  3. **Rabbit** (Letter **R**) — `/ɹæb.ɪt/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to hear rhotic resonance: *"R says /r/... rrr-red! Red!"*.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Name & Letter Station** — Audio modeling of rhotic tongue bunching.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Sound Bucket** — Sorting /r/ words (*red*, *rabbit*) from /w/ words (*water*, *wind*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Build & Say** — Assembling `R + ED -> RED`. Child repeats with model.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Forest Discovery** — Finding the red rabbit independently.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Talk With Buddy** — *"I have a red ball."*, *"The rabbit can hop."*

---
