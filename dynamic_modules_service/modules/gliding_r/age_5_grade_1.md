# Learning Module: Gliding of /r/ (Grade 1 / Age 5)
**Module ID**: `gliding-r-age-5-grade-1`  
**Target Process**: Gliding of /r/ (/r/ -> [w])  
**Target Age / Grade**: Age 5 (60–71 months) | Grade 1  
**Clinical Status**: Expected Developmental Error (Suppression Milestone ~6;0)  
**Educational Standard**: DepEd MATATAG Grade 1 Literacy (`RL1PA-I-5`, `RL1PWS-I-1`, `RL1PWS-I-5`)  
**Island Gameplay Map**: Grade 1 Island 1: *"Family & Community Discovery"* (Level 1.3 Community Walk)  

---

## 1. Level 1.1: Name & Letter Station Framework
- **MATATAG Focus**: `RL1PWS-I-1` (Letter R sounds), Community vocabulary.
- **How It Works**: Three Grade 1 images appear:
  1. **Red** (Letter **R**) — `/ɹɛd/`
  2. **Ring** (Letter **R**) — `/ɹɪŋ/`
  3. **Run** (Letter **R**) — `/ɹʌn/`
- **Interaction**: Child taps all 3 to isolate /r/ without lip rounding.

---

## 2. 5-Stage Gameplay Scaffolding
- **Stage 1 (Listen)**: **1.1 Sound Station** — Acoustic discrimination of /r/ vs /w/.
- **Stage 2 (Recognize)**: **1.2 Family Rhyme Porch** — Identifying rhyming /r/ words (*red*, *bed*).
- **Stage 3 (Guided Say)**: **1.3 Community Walk** — Modeled guided path repetition.
- **Stage 4 (Independent Say)**: **1.4 Silly Monster** — Monster says *"I see a wed car"* -> Child fixes: *"red!"*.
- **Stage 5 (Use in Context)**: **1.5 Family Story Time** — *"My brother drives a red truck."*, *"The rabbit runs in the park."*

---

- **Phrases**: `a red truck`, `a little rabbit`, `run in the park`
