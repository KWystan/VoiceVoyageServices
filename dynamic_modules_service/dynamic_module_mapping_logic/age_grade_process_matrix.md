## 2. Summary by DepEd Grade Level

### Kindergarten (Age 4;0 – 4;11)
- **Primary Educational Focus**: Oral language foundational sounds, initial phoneme identification (MATATAG Competencies 7, 8, 15, 23).
- **Expected Errors**: Stopping (/s, z, v, ʃ, θ, ð/), Velar Fronting (/k, ɡ/), Palatal Fronting (/ʃ/), Deaffrication (/tʃ, dʒ/), Gliding (/l, r/), Cluster Reduction (2 & 3 element), Weak Syllable Deletion, Devoicing.
- **Delayed Errors**: Final Consonant Deletion (FCD), Prevocalic Voicing, Reduplication, Consonant Harmony, Stopping of /f/.
- **Atypical (Red Flags)**: Initial Consonant Deletion (ICD), Medial Consonant Deletion (MCD), Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization.

### Grade 1 (Ages 5;0 – 5;11 & 6;0 – 6;11)
- **Primary Educational Focus**: Letter-sound correspondence, onset-rime blending, basic CVC & CVCC words (MATATAG `RL1PA`, `RL1PWS`).
- **Expected Errors**: Gliding of /r/, Stopping of late fricatives (/v, θ, ð, ʒ/), 3-element Cluster Reduction, Vowelization.
- **Delayed Errors**: Stopping of /f, s, z/, Velar Fronting, Palatal Fronting, Deaffrication, Gliding of /l/, 2-element Cluster Reduction, Weak Syllable Deletion, Final Consonant Deletion, Devoicing, Voicing.
- **Atypical (Red Flags)**: ICD, MCD, Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization.

### Grade 2 (Ages 6;0 – 7;11)
- **Primary Educational Focus**: Consonant digraphs, complex blends, multi-syllable decoding (MATATAG `EN2PA`, `EN2PWS`).
- **Expected Errors**: Stopping of late interdental fricatives (/θ, ð/).
- **Delayed Errors**: Gliding of /l, r/, Stopping of /f, s, z, v, ʃ/, Velar/Palatal Fronting, Deaffrication, Cluster Reduction (all blends), Weak Syllable Deletion, Final Consonant Deletion, Vowelization, Voicing errors.
- **Atypical (Red Flags)**: ICD, MCD, Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization.

### Grade 3 (Age 8;0+)
- **Primary Educational Focus**: Advanced word structures (CCVC, CVCC, VCV, VCCV), reading fluency (MATATAG `EN3PWS`).
- **Expected Errors**: None. All 24 English consonant phonemes are 90% mastered by 84–96 months.
- **Delayed Errors**: Any lingering developmental phonological process is clinically delayed and prioritized for remedial intervention.
- **Atypical (Red Flags)**: ICD, MCD, Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization.
