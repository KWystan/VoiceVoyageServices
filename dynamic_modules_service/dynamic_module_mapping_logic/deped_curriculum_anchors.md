## 2. Detailed Grade Level Mapping

### 2.1 Kindergarten (Age 4;0 – 4;11)
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: Foundational Oral Language and Phonological Awareness.
- **MATATAG Competencies**:
  - **Competency 7**: Produce the sound of the letter it stands for.
  - **Competency 8**: Identify the letters in the alphabet (mother tongue / English).
  - **Competency 15**: Segment a two- to three-syllable word into syllables.
  - **Competency 23**: Identify the beginning sound of a given word.
- **Phonological Targets**:
  - Word shapes: Single syllables (CV, VC, simple CVC).
  - High-frequency vocabulary: *sun, cat, dog, bed, pig, bus, cup*.
- **Gameplay Islands**:
  - Island 1: Sound Discovery (Syllables)
  - Island 2: Word Explorer (Single CVC words)
  - Island 3: Phrase Builder (2-word phrases: *"big sun"*)
  - Island 4: Sentence Quest (3-word sentences: *"I see sun"*)
  - Island 5: Mastery Arena (High-repetition review)

### 2.2 Grade 1 (Ages 5;0 – 5;11 & 6;0 – 6;11)
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: Reading Literacy — Letter-Sound Correspondence & Word Recognition.
- **MATATAG Competencies**:
  - **`RL1PA-I-1`**: Blend phonemes to produce simple words (CVC, CCVC, CVCC).
  - **`RL1PA-I-2`**: Segment words into individual phonemes.
  - **`RL1PWS-I-1`**: Read grade-level words with consonant blends and digraphs.
- **Phonological Targets**:
  - Word shapes: CVC, CVCC (*milk, hand*), CCVC (*star, frog*).
  - Phoneme mastery: /r/, /l/, /s/, /z/, /tʃ/, /dʒ/.
- **Gameplay Islands**:
  - Expanded lexical items with blending and syllable clapping mechanics.

### 2.3 Grade 2 (Ages 6;0 – 7;11)
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: English Language Arts — Advanced Decoding & Fluency.
- **MATATAG Competencies**:
  - **`EN2PA-II-1`**: Manipulate individual sounds in words (addition, deletion, substitution).
  - **`EN2PWS-II-1`**: Read 2-syllable words with consonant blends and vowel digraphs (*ea, oa, ai*).
  - **`EN2PWS-II-2`**: Read high-frequency sight words with inflectional endings (*-s, -ed, -ing*).
- **Phonological Targets**:
  - Word shapes: CCVC, CVCC, multi-syllable words (*rabbit, jumping, yellow*).
  - Interdental fricatives: /θ/ (*thumb*), /ð/ (*feather*).

### 2.4 Grade 3 (Age 8;0+)
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: English Language Arts — Complex Word Analysis & Text Fluency.
- **MATATAG Competencies**:
  - **`EN3PWS-III-1`**: Read multi-syllabic words with complex affixes and roots.
  - **`EN3PWS-III-2`**: Read grade-level passages with accuracy, appropriate rate, and expression.
- **Phonological Targets**:
  - Word shapes: Multi-syllabic CVCV/CVCC/CCVCC words (*umbrella, computer, elephant*).
  - Full phonetic automaticity across all 24 English consonants in connected speech.
