## 1. Age 4 (48–59 Months) — Kindergarten Profile

### 1.1 Consonant Inventory (90% Mastery Threshold)
- **Mastered Consonants (Ages 2 to 4)**:  
  **/p, b, m, d, n, h, w, t, k, ɡ, f, v, ŋ, j, l, dʒ, tʃ, s, ʃ, z/** (20 of 24 consonants).
- **Later Developing Consonants**: `/r/` (~5;0–6;0), `/ʒ/` (~5;6), `/ð/` (~6;0), `/θ/` (~7;0–8;0).
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: Kindergarten Oral Language & Phonological Awareness (MATATAG Competencies 7, 8, 15, 23). Word structures: CV, VC, CVC.

### 1.2 Clinical Process Breakdown

```
Age 4 Status:
├── 1. Expected Errors (Developmentally Normal)
│   ├── Stopping of /s, z, v, ʃ, ʒ, θ, ð/ ("tun" for sun, "doo" for zoo, "tum" for thumb)
│   ├── Gliding of /l, r/ ("wook" for look, "wed" for red, "yewow" for yellow)
│   ├── Cluster Reduction (2-el & 3-el) ("tar" for star, "boo" for blue, "fog" for frog)
│   ├── Deaffrication ("share" or "tar" for chair, "deep" for jeep)
│   ├── Palatal Fronting ("soo" for shoe, "fis" for fish)
│   ├── Velar Fronting [Transitioning] ("tar" for car, "dote" for goat)
│   ├── Weak Syllable Deletion [Transitioning] ("nana" for banana)
│   └── Devoicing of Codas [Transitioning] ("bet" for bed, "pick" for pig)
│
├── 2. Delayed Processes (Should Be Suppressed Before Age 4)
│   ├── Final Consonant Deletion (FCD) [Suppressed ~3;0–3;3] ("ca" for cat, "daw" for dog)
│   ├── Prevocalic Voicing [Suppressed ~3;0] ("bye" for pie, "den" for ten)
│   ├── Reduplication [Suppressed ~2;6–3;0] ("wawa" for water, "baba" for bottle)
│   ├── Consonant Harmony [Suppressed ~3;0–3;6] ("guck" for duck, "pap" for cap)
│   └── Stopping of /f/ [Suppressed ~3;6] ("pan" for fan, "pish" for fish)
│
└── 3. Red Flag / Atypical Processes (Never Typical at Any Age)
    ├── Initial Consonant Deletion (ICD) ("ig" for pig, "all" for ball)
    ├── Medial Consonant Deletion (MCD) ("payer" for paper, "wah-er" for water)
    ├── Backing ("gog" for dog, "ken" for ten, "koe" for toe)
    ├── Liquidization ("roter" for water, "lellow" for yellow)
    ├── Frication ("soo" for two, "cuff" for cup)
    └── Denasalization ("boon" for moon, "dice" for nice)
```

## 2. Age 5 (60–71 Months) — Grade 1 Entry Profile

### 2.1 Consonant Inventory (90% Mastery Threshold)
- **Mastered Consonants (Ages 2 to 5)**:  
  All Age 4 consonants + **/r/** (rhotic liquid) and **/ʒ/** (voiced palatal fricative, *measure*, *treasure*).
- **Later Developing Consonants**: `/ð/` (*this*, *feather* ~6;0) and `/θ/` (*thumb*, *bath* ~7;0–8;0).
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: Grade 1 Reading Literacy — Letter-Sound Correspondence, Onset-Rime, and CVC/CVCC Blending (`RL1PA`, `RL1PWS`).

### 2.2 Clinical Process Breakdown

```
Age 5 Status:
├── 1. Expected Errors (Developmentally Normal)
│   ├── Gliding of /r/ ("wed" for red, "wabbit" for rabbit, "wing" for ring)
│   ├── Stopping of Late Fricatives (/v, ʒ, θ, ð/) ("berry" for very, "tum" for thumb)
│   ├── 3-Element Cluster Reduction (/str, spr, skr/ → [t, p, k]) ("teet" for street)
│   └── Vowelization of Post-Vocalic Liquids ("baw" for ball, "kah" for car)
│
├── 2. Delayed Processes (Clinically Significant Delay at Age 5)
│   ├── Stopping of /f, s, z/ [Suppressed ~3;6–4;6] ("tun" for sun, "pan" for fan)
│   ├── Velar Fronting [Suppressed ~3;6–4;0] ("tar" for car, "tup" for cup)
│   ├── Palatal Fronting [Suppressed ~4;6–5;0] ("soo" for shoe, "fis" for fish)
│   ├── Deaffrication [Suppressed ~4;6] ("share" for chair, "zeep" for jeep)
│   ├── Gliding of /l/ [Suppressed ~5;0] ("wook" for look, "yewow" for yellow)
│   ├── 2-Element Cluster Reduction [Suppressed ~4;0] ("tar" for star, "boo" for blue)
│   ├── Weak Syllable Deletion [Suppressed ~4;0] ("nana" for banana)
│   ├── Final Consonant Deletion [Suppressed ~3;0–3;3] ("ca" for cat)
│   └── Prevocalic Voicing / Devoicing [Suppressed ~3;0–4;0] ("bye" for pie, "bet" for bed)
│
└── 3. Red Flag / Atypical Processes (Immediate SLP Referral)
    └── ICD, MCD, Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization
```

## 3. Age 6 (72–83 Months) — Grade 1 / Grade 2 Profile

### 3.1 Consonant Inventory (90% Mastery Threshold)
- **Mastered Consonants (Ages 2 to 6)**:  
  All Age 2–5 consonants + **/ð/** ("voiced th" as in *this*, *that*, *feather*, *breathe*).
- **Later Developing Consonants**: `/θ/` ("voiceless th" as in *thumb*, *teeth*, *math* ~7;0–8;0).
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: Grade 1 / Grade 2 English — Consonant Digraphs, Initial/Final Blends, and Two-Syllable Decoding (`RL1PWS`, `EN2PA`, `EN2PWS`).

### 3.2 Clinical Process Breakdown

```
Age 6 Status:
├── 1. Expected Errors (Developmentally Normal)
│   ├── Stopping of Interdental Fricatives (/θ, ð/ → [t, d]) ("tum" for thumb, "dis" for this)
│   └── Vowelization [Late Transition ~5;0–6;0] ("baw" for ball, "miyok" for milk)
│
├── 2. Delayed Processes (Clinically Significant Delay at Age 6)
│   ├── Gliding of /r/ [Suppressed ~6;0] ("wed" for red, "wun" for run)
│   ├── Gliding of /l/ [Suppressed ~5;0] ("wook" for look, "yight" for light)
│   ├── Stopping of Sibilants & Palatals (/s, z, v, ʃ, ʒ/) ("tun" for sun, "doo" for zoo)
│   ├── Velar & Palatal Fronting [Suppressed ~4;0–5;0] ("tar" for car, "soo" for shoe)
│   ├── Deaffrication [Suppressed ~4;6] ("share" for chair, "deep" for jeep)
│   ├── Cluster Reduction (All 2-el & 3-el blends) [Suppressed ~4;0–5;0] ("tar" for star)
│   ├── Weak Syllable Deletion [Suppressed ~4;0] ("nana" for banana, "pyuter" for computer)
│   ├── Final Consonant Deletion [Suppressed ~3;0–3;3] ("ca" for cat, "beh" for bed)
│   └── Voicing & Devoicing Errors [Suppressed ~3;0–4;0] ("bye" for pie, "pick" for pig)
│
└── 3. Red Flag / Atypical Processes (Immediate SLP Referral)
    └── ICD, MCD, Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization
```

## 4. Age 7 (84–95 Months) — Grade 2 Profile

### 4.1 Consonant Inventory (90% Mastery Threshold)
- **Mastered Consonants (Ages 2 to 7)**:  
  23 of 24 consonants fully mastered. The only phoneme still completing acquisition is **/θ/** (*thumb*, *bath*).
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: Grade 2 English — Multi-syllable decoding, consonant clusters, vowel digraphs, inflectional endings (`EN2PWS`, `EN2PA`).

### 4.2 Clinical Process Breakdown

```
Age 7 Status:
├── 1. Expected Errors (Developmentally Normal)
│   └── Stopping of Voiceless /θ/ ("tum" for thumb, "bæt" for bath, "teet" for teeth)
│
├── 2. Delayed Processes (Clinically Significant Delay at Age 7)
│   ├── Gliding of /l, r/ [Suppressed ~5;0–6;0] ("wed" for red, "wook" for look)
│   ├── Stopping of All Other Fricatives (/f, v, s, z, ʃ, ʒ, ð/) ("tun" for sun, "dis" for this)
│   ├── Velar & Palatal Fronting [Suppressed ~4;0–5;0] ("tar" for car, "soo" for shoe)
│   ├── Deaffrication [Suppressed ~4;6] ("share" for chair, "deep" for jeep)
│   ├── Cluster Reduction (All blends) [Suppressed ~4;0–5;0] ("tar" for star, "fog" for frog)
│   ├── Weak Syllable Deletion [Suppressed ~4;0] ("nana" for banana)
│   ├── Final Consonant Deletion [Suppressed ~3;0–3;3] ("ca" for cat)
│   ├── Vowelization [Suppressed ~5;0–6;0] ("baw" for ball, "kah" for car)
│   └── Voicing & Devoicing Errors [Suppressed ~3;0–4;0] ("bye" for pie, "bet" for bed)
│
└── 3. Red Flag / Atypical Processes (Immediate SLP Referral)
    └── ICD, MCD, Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization
```

## 5. Age 8 (96+ Months) — Grade 3 Profile

### 5.1 Consonant Inventory (90% Mastery Threshold)
- **Mastered Consonants**: **ALL 24 ENGLISH CONSONANTS (100% MASTERY)**.  
  By 96 months (8 years), typical speech sound acquisition is clinically complete.
- **DepEd Educational Anchor**: Grade 3 English — Complex multi-syllabic morphological decoding (CCVC, CVCC, VCV, VCCV), reading fluency, and connected speech automaticity (`EN3PWS`).

### 5.2 Clinical Process Breakdown

```
Age 8 Status:
├── 1. Expected Errors
│   └── NONE. (All developmental speech sound processes are clinically suppressed by Age 8).
│
├── 2. Delayed Processes (High-Priority Remedial Intervention at Age 8)
│   ├── Gliding of /l, r/ (Persistent liquid substitution)
│   ├── Stopping of Any Fricative (/s, z, f, v, ʃ, ʒ, θ, ð/)
│   ├── Fronting of Velars or Palatals (/k, ɡ, ʃ/)
│   ├── Deaffrication of Affricates (/tʃ, dʒ/)
│   ├── Cluster Reduction (Lingering blend omissions in CCVC/CVCC words)
│   ├── Weak Syllable Deletion (Relabeled "Syllable Reduction" in Grade 3 multi-syllabic words)
│   ├── Final Consonant Deletion (Damages morphological markers like past -ed and plural -s)
│   ├── Vowelization (Post-vocalic liquid omissions)
│   └── Voicing / Devoicing Errors
│
└── 3. Red Flag / Atypical Processes (Immediate SLP Comprehensive Assessment)
    └── ICD, MCD, Backing, Liquidization, Frication, Denasalization
```
